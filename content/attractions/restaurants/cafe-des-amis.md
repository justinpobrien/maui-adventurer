Cafe des Amis occupies an unusual culinary niche in Paia, pairing French and Mediterranean-style crepes with home-style Indian curries on a single menu — a combination that shouldn't work as well as it does. Savory crepes filled with meats, vegetables, and curry, alongside sweet dessert versions, sit next to curry wraps and seafood stew, giving diners a genuinely different set of options than the seafood-heavy restaurants dominating this stretch of Baldwin Avenue.

Seating is available both indoors and in an outdoor courtyard, where live music plays every evening, adding to the relaxed, unhurried feel that distinguishes this from Paia's more counter-service-oriented spots. A happy hour from 4 to 6pm offers discounted beer, wine, and cocktails.

Hours run 11am to 8:30pm Monday through Friday, and 9am to 8:30pm on weekends, making it flexible enough for a late breakfast, lunch, or dinner depending on when a Paia stop happens to fall in the day.
