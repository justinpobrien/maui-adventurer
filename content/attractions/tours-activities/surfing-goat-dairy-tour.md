Surfing Goat Dairy in Kula is the only goat farm on Maui offering public tours and cheese tastings, making it a distinctive upcountry stop especially popular with families. The farm produces a range of award-winning goat cheeses, and tours combine a walk through the pastures with a tasting of several cheese varieties at the end.

Two tour formats are offered: a 30-minute Casual Dairy Tour priced at $21 for adults and $15 for children, and a longer 60-minute Milking Tour at $28 for adults and $25 for children that includes hands-on time actually milking a goat — a hit with younger kids in particular.

The farm operates Tuesday through Saturday, closed Sundays and Mondays year-round (worth double-checking directly, since some older listings still show different Sunday hours). Reservations are strongly recommended, as tours fill up quickly, especially the smaller-capacity milking tour.
