Sea House Restaurant at Napili Kai Beach Resort offers full-day dining with a genuinely strong ocean view over Napili Bay, running breakfast, lunch, happy hour, and dinner in clearly defined windows throughout the day. Breakfast standouts include the Molokai Sweet Potato Frittata, Haleakala Pancake, and a homemade macadamia nut cinnamon roll, while dinner leans on dishes like macadamia nut-crusted mahi mahi and poke nachos.

The restaurant's early-bird special is a particularly strong value for a resort setting: from 5:30 to 6pm, a three-course meal (salad, tropical dessert, and a choice of macadamia-crusted fish, filet mignon, or shrimp scampi) runs $38 — a meaningful discount compared to full a la carte dinner pricing.

Happy hour, running 2 to 4:45pm, offers appetizers in the $6-8 range, including Kula onion soup, coconut shrimp, and seared ahi. Between the early-bird deal and happy hour, Sea House offers more accessible pricing windows than many similarly scenic resort restaurants.
