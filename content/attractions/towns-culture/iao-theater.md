The Iao Theater, opened in 1928 in Spanish Mission style, was once Wailuku's main entertainment venue and remains a genuine historic landmark at the heart of the town's arts district. Since 2009, the nonprofit Maui OnStage has presented monthly theatrical performances here, keeping the century-old building in active use rather than letting it become a static museum piece.

The calendar mixes local community theater productions with occasional touring acts — recent bookings have included well-known touring performers alongside Maui OnStage's own regular programming. Checking the current schedule directly (via mauionstage.com or the box office) is essential since programming rotates and isn't a fixed daily draw.

Outside of ticketed events, the building's general hours are limited and vary by day — Tuesday through Thursday 9am to 5pm, Friday and Saturday evenings 6 to 10pm, Sunday 2 to 6pm, closed Monday — making this a venue best planned around a specific show rather than a casual drop-in visit.
