Maui Nui Botanical Gardens in Kahului takes a distinctive approach compared to Maui's more tropical-themed gardens: it focuses specifically on conserving native Hawaiian plants and those brought by early Polynesian voyagers, giving visitors a genuinely different, culturally grounded perspective on the island's plant life.

The garden is open Tuesday through Saturday from 8am to 4pm (with occasional closures for weather or state holidays), and admission runs $10 for general visitors, with free entry for members, kama'aina with valid Hawaii ID, and children under 12 — making it an accessible, budget-friendly stop for families. The location at 150 Kanaloa Avenue sits directly across from War Memorial Stadium in Kahului, easy to combine with other central Maui errands or stops.

Given its educational focus on native ecology and Hawaiian cultural plant use, it's a solid choice for families looking to add some substance to a garden visit beyond simple sightseeing, and pairs well with a stop at nearby Kepaniwai Heritage Gardens for a broader look at Maui's cultural and natural history.
