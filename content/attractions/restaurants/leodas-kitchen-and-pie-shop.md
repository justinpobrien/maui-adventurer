Leoda's Kitchen and Pie Shop has built a devoted following around its combination of hearty sandwiches and an outstanding pie selection, all served from a small, casual counter-service spot in Olowalu village between Lahaina and Ma'alaea. Reuben sandwiches with house-made sauerkraut and hot dogs topped with poha berry mustard and pineapple chutney anchor the savory side of the menu.

The real draw for many visitors is the pie case: Banana Cream, Chocolate Mac Nut, Coconut Cream, Olowalu Lime, Apple Crumb, Peanut Butter, and Chocolate Haupia pies rotate through, alongside fresh-baked bread and crisp salads topped with charred Brussels sprouts for anyone wanting something lighter.

Open daily from 10am to 6pm, its location along the main highway between West Maui and central/south Maui makes it a natural stop for road trips in either direction rather than a dedicated destination requiring a special detour.
