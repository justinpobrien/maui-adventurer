Waiʻanapanapa's black sand beach and sea cliffs make it one of the most photographed stops on the Road to Hana, framed by a short trail along the lava coastline.

A reservation system limits crowding, so it's worth booking a timed entry ahead of the drive rather than hoping to walk in. The beach itself has a strong shore break, so most visitors stick to wading and photos rather than swimming.
