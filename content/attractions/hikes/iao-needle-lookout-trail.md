Iao Needle Lookout Trail is a short, paved walk inside Iao Valley State Monument leading to a lookout point facing Kuka'emoku, better known as Iao Needle — a striking 1,200-foot volcanic spire wrapped in green that holds deep significance in Hawaiian history as the site of a decisive 1790 battle fought by Kamehameha I. Combined with the adjoining ethnobotanical loop, the full walk covers roughly 0.6 miles and is rated easy, suitable for most visitors including families with young kids.

Entry now requires an advance reservation for non-Hawaii residents, bookable through the state's official portal, along with fees of $10 per vehicle for parking and $5 per person for entry (children under three free; Hawaii residents with valid state ID enter free). As of mid-2026, the park had been under a temporary closure for safety improvements through late June — worth double-checking that it has reopened and confirming current fees before building a visit around it.

Once inside, the walk itself is unhurried and scenic rather than physically demanding, passing tropical plantings and stream views on the way to the main overlook — a good complement to a Wailuku or central Maui day rather than a full excursion on its own.
