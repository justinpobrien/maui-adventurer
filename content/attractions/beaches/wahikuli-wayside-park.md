Wahikuli Wayside Park is an 8.3-acre stretch of beachfront between Lahaina and Ka'anapali that functions more as a community picnic ground than a headline snorkeling destination — and that's part of its appeal. With 25 covered pavilions, eight barbecue grills, and nine uncovered tables, it's built for groups who want shade and a place to eat rather than a full day of sand.

The swimming area is calm and sandy, suitable for casual dips and light snorkeling, though it doesn't have the reef density of spots like Kapalua or Olowalu. What it does have is accessibility: ADA parking, pathways, and restrooms are part of the park's design, along with three restroom pavilions, outdoor showers, and drinking fountains spread along the grounds.

Open daily from 7am to 8pm, Wahikuli is a good pick for a low-key stop — a lunch break on a Road-to-Hana-adjacent day, a quiet swim without resort crowds, or a rest point along the walkable stretch between Lahaina and the Ka'anapali beachwalk.
