Maui Brewing Company's Kihei location combines a full working brewery with a genuinely complete restaurant menu — snacks, salads, pizzas, sandwiches, burgers, entrees, and a children's menu — rather than functioning as just a beer hall with limited bar food. It's Maui's best-known craft brewery, and this Lipoa Parkway location serves as its flagship taproom and restaurant.

The beer selection unsurprisingly centers on the brewery's own local craft lineup, and a daily happy hour from 3 to 5pm sweetens the deal with $2 off draft beers, $3 off craft cocktails, and $14 pizzas. A separate, more limited-menu Tasting Room operates alongside the main restaurant for those wanting a quicker beer-focused visit.

Open daily 11am to 10pm, it's a solid family-friendly option given the kids' menu, while still functioning well as an evening spot for beer enthusiasts wanting to try the brewery's range on-site.
