Twin Falls is the most accessible waterfall stop on the Road to Hana, a short walk from a farm-stand parking area at around mile marker 2.

A network of short trails leads to several pools and cascades of varying size; the closest are a five-minute walk, with quieter pools another ten to fifteen minutes further in. It's a popular first stop, so early arrival helps avoid the crowds that build through the morning.
