Da Kitchen is a long-running local plate lunch spot known for serving generous, filling portions of Hawaiian and local-style comfort food — think chicken katsu, tempura mahi mahi, teriyaki beef, Korean-style short ribs, and the full Hawaiian Plate (pork lau lau, kalua pork, chicken long rice, and lomi salmon), all served with the standard two scoops of rice and macaroni salad.

There's a genuine location question worth flagging here: some current sources point to a Kahului location at Triangle Square (425 Koloa St), while the restaurant's own active website is branded for a Kihei location. This suggests either a relocation, a second location, or outdated listings still circulating online — worth a quick call or a check of the current official site before planning a specific stop.

Regardless of exact address, the appeal is consistent: large, no-frills plate lunches at reasonable prices, squarely aimed at satisfying rather than impressing.
