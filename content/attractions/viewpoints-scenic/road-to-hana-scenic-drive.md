The Road to Hana is Maui's signature scenic drive: 50 miles of the Hana Highway winding along the island's wet, green windward coast, threading through 620 curves and across 59 one-lane bridges as it passes waterfalls, black sand beaches, and dense rainforest. It's less a single destination than a full-day itinerary built around dozens of possible stops.

Driven nonstop, the one-way trip from Kahului to Hana takes 2.5 to 3 hours, but almost nobody drives it that way — most visitors budget a full 10 to 12 hour day, filling it with waterfall hikes, lookout stops, and roadside fruit stands along the way. One-lane bridges require basic etiquette: yield to any vehicle already on the bridge and wait your turn, since ignoring this causes most of the close calls reported on the highway.

Practical planning matters here more than on almost any other Maui drive: fill the gas tank in Paia before setting out, download offline maps since cell service disappears around mile marker 15, bring cash for cash-only roadside stands, and start early (6-7am) to get ahead of both tour traffic and the midday clouds that often roll in over the east side. Wai'anapanapa State Park now requires an advance reservation ($5/person plus $10/vehicle) if a stop there is part of the plan.
