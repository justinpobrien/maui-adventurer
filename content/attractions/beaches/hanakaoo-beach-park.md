Locally known as Canoe Beach for the outrigger canoe clubs based here, Hanaka'o'o Beach Park sits at the southern end of Ka'anapali and offers what that stretch of coast is otherwise short on: ample, free parking. A large lot near the entrance usually has space even when the paid resort-area lots up the beachwalk are full, with an overflow dirt lot near the cemetery as a backup on busy weekends or during canoe events.

The swimming here is calm and family-friendly, with lifeguards on duty during daylight hours — a meaningful plus for parents who want a supervised alternative to the unguarded stretches of beach further along. Picnic pavilions, restrooms, and showers round out a park that's as functional as it is scenic, and a canoe storage shed serves the paddling clubs that give the beach its nickname.

Because it connects directly to the paved Ka'anapali beachwalk, Hanaka'o'o works well as a park-once base: leave the car here, swim or relax at Canoe Beach, then walk the 10-15 minutes north to Black Rock, Whalers Village, or the main Ka'anapali beach scene without moving the car again.
