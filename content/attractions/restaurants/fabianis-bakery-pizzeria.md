Fabiani's combines a wood-fired pizzeria with a proper Italian bakery under one roof, offering handmade pastas, pizzas, and fresh salads alongside a dessert case stocked with tiramisu, fruit tarts, and cannoli. Breakfast and brunch options — benedicts, omelets, pastries, and espresso drinks — extend the restaurant's usefulness beyond just dinner.

Hours are genuinely uneven across the week: Monday through Thursday the restaurant doesn't open until 2pm, running to 8pm, while Friday and Saturday open earlier at 11am and run to 9pm, and Sunday runs 11am to 8pm. This makes early-week lunch visits impossible and worth double-checking before planning around this stop.

A daily happy hour from 2 to 5pm at the bar offers discounted food and drinks, giving a reasonable window to combine a lighter meal with the bakery case desserts.
