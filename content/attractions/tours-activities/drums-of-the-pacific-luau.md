Drums of the Pacific Luau at the Hyatt Regency Maui in Ka'anapali offers a full luau experience beginning with a mai tai greeting and kukui nut lei, followed by pre-show entertainment, a torch-lighting ceremony, an all-you-can-eat buffet dinner, an open bar with tropical cocktails, and a live Polynesian revue that closes with a three-man fireknife dance finale.

Standard pricing starts around $195 per adult (regular rate closer to $220), with VIP seating running significantly higher at roughly $249 plus tax for adults and $125 for children. The show runs five nights a week — Sunday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday — rather than every night, so checking the calendar before planning around a specific date matters.

Check-in and doors shift seasonally: summer hours (May 15-August 31) check in at 6pm with 6:15pm doors, while winter hours (September 1-May 14) move earlier to 5pm check-in and 5:15pm doors. Being at Ka'anapali rather than Lahaina town, this venue was unaffected by the 2023 wildfire and has operated on a normal schedule throughout.
