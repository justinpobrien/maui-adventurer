Haleakala's summit-to-sea elevation drop makes it a natural setting for downhill cycling tours, and this remains a well-established, actively operating category on Maui. Options range from fully guided group rides with a support van to self-guided rentals where cyclists set their own pace, with pricing spanning roughly $109 for shorter express routes up to $320 for full guided sunrise-to-breakfast packages.

Separately, e-bike tours are offered in lower-elevation areas like Lahaina and Kihei, giving riders an easier, flatter alternative to the steep volcano descent — a different product from the Haleakala downhill rides.

One flag worth noting directly: research did not turn up confirmed, currently-operating Segway tours specifically tied to Haleakala. General Segway tours exist elsewhere on Maui, but given the summit's access restrictions and permit requirements, this combination should be verified directly with a specific operator before being treated as a bookable activity — don't assume availability based on general Maui Segway tour listings.
