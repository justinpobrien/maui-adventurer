Pipiwai Trail climbs through a dramatic bamboo forest to 400-foot Waimoku Falls, in the Kīpahulu section of Haleakalā National Park near the end of the Road to Hana.

The out-and-back trail is about 4 miles round trip, passing a giant banyan tree, a boardwalk through towering bamboo, and several smaller waterfalls before reaching Waimoku. It gains real elevation and can be slippery, but doesn't require technical hiking experience.
