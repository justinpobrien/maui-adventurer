Nalu's South Shore Grill covers a lot of ground with a genuinely large menu — 55 items spread across 13 sections including all-day breakfast, benedicts and omelets, and a "super foods" section for lighter, health-focused options. Seared ahi, crab cakes, cold tofu salad, and the signature Cane Fire chicken skewers are among the standout dishes that come up repeatedly in reviews.

Hours run long, roughly 8am to 9-10pm daily depending on the source, making it flexible for breakfast, lunch, or dinner without needing to plan around a narrow window. A daily happy hour from 3 to 6pm adds value with $5 well drinks and margaritas, $7 pupus from a dedicated small-plates menu, and discounted wine by the glass.

Located in Kihei, it works well as a reliable, sit-down option for groups who can't agree on what they want — the sheer menu breadth means there's likely something for everyone from health-conscious eaters to those wanting a full plate of local comfort food.
