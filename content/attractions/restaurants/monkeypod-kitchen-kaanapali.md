Monkeypod Kitchen by Merriman brings a casual-upscale, beer-and-pizza-forward concept to Whalers Village in Ka'anapali — worth noting since some older references place this restaurant in nearby Kapalua rather than its actual Ka'anapali location at 2435 Kaanapali Parkway.

The menu centers on fresh island fish and hand-tossed pizzas from a kiawe wood-burning oven, alongside standout dishes like taro ravioli and the Maui Cattle Company cheeseburger. The signature pineapple pork pizza is a frequent highlight in reviews. A serious craft beer program — 36 taps, each served at a precise 29 degrees — pairs with culinary cocktails built around ingredients like acai spirit, coconut water, and Kula lavender.

Open daily 11am to 10pm, it's a solid choice for a lively, moderately priced dinner within walking distance of Ka'anapali Beach, particularly for groups who want good beer alongside their meal.
