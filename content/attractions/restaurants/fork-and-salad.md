Fork & Salad fills the healthy, fast-casual niche in Kihei's dining scene, built around locally-sourced salads, soups, sandwiches, and kombucha served quickly — the restaurant advertises orders ready in three minutes or less. A build-your-own-salad option with more than 50 ingredients makes it flexible for groups with different tastes or dietary needs, while set combinations like lemon-lime chicken, smoky papaya chicken with goat cheese, and falafel quinoa cover those who'd rather not build from scratch.

Soup options include tomato bisque and clam chowder, giving the menu a bit of range beyond just salads, and the overall positioning — quick, fresh, not heavy — makes it a good counterpoint to Kihei's many fried-fish and plate-lunch options.

Open daily 11am to 8pm at its original Kihei location in the Azeka Mauka shopping center, with a second location in Kahului at 120 Hookele Street for visitors staying closer to the airport.
