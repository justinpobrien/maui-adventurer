Makamaka'ole Falls, often called the Enchanted Forest for its lush, moss-draped setting, is one of the only waterfalls in West Maui reachable on foot — a real point of distinction on a coast better known for beaches and snorkeling than waterfall hikes. It sits in the West Maui Forest Reserve above Wailuku, reached via the 13 Crossings Trail, a roughly 2-mile round trip that's relatively easy in terms of grade but involves multiple stream crossings along the way.

The access situation carries a genuine legal ambiguity worth understanding before heading out: the trail and footbridge likely cross land owned by East Maui Irrigation (EMI), a private entity, meaning technically this could be considered trespassing. In practice, there are no documented reports of hikers facing enforcement action, but that's not the same as official permission, and visitors should weigh that gray area for themselves — or contact Hawaii's DLNR directly for the most current guidance.

Assuming conditions are safe, the payoff is a genuinely atmospheric hike through dense, fern-covered forest with multiple stream crossings leading to the falls itself. Because those crossings depend on manageable water levels, this is not a hike to attempt after heavy rain, when stream flow can rise quickly and crossings become unsafe.
