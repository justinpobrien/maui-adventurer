Baldwin Beach Park, just outside Paia on Maui's north shore, is a full-service county park built around one of the island's best bodysurfing and bodyboarding beaches. Open lawns, soccer and baseball fields, a covered pavilion, and full restroom/shower facilities make it as much a community park as a beach destination.

The main stretch of sand faces open ocean and gets real waves, which is the draw for bodysurfers and bodyboarders but also the reason winter conditions can turn genuinely hazardous — strong shorebreak has been known to make swimming and bodyboarding dangerous in that season. Lifeguards are stationed at the Paia end of the beach during operating hours, adding a layer of safety not every north shore beach offers.

For families with younger kids, the better move is heading to the protected coves on either end — Baldwin Cove and "Baby Beach" — where calmer water makes for safer, more relaxed swimming than the open middle stretch.
