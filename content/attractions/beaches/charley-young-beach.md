Charley Young Beach is the low-key cousin of the busier Kamaole beaches just next door — a smaller, quieter pocket of sand that locals often prefer precisely because it draws fewer crowds. The swimming here is calm and easy, though there's no lifeguard, so it suits confident swimmers and supervised kids more than a completely hands-off beach day.

Getting to the sand involves a short ramp and a set of concrete steps down from the access point, and amenities are minimal — a portable toilet, showers, and trash bins, without the full build-out of the Kamaole parks. Parking is a block away on Kaiau Place, opposite the Maui Vista condos, and it fills quickly, but overflow options exist at nearby South Kihei Road and Kamaole Beach Park I.

Because Charley Young sits right next to Kam I along the same stretch of sand, the two effectively function as one long beach — worth remembering if the small Kaiau Place lot is full and Kam I's has space instead.
