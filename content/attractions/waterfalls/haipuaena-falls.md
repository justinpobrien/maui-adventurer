Haipua'ena Falls is one of the quieter waterfall stops on the Hana Highway, tucked away just past mile marker 11 and easy to miss without knowing exactly where to look. A short trail — about 100 yards — leads from the roadside to a small, generally calm pool at the base of the falls, framed by ferns and wild ginger in a setting that feels more secluded than the bigger-name stops nearby.

Because there's no dedicated parking area at the falls themselves, the practical approach is to park at the nearby Waikamoi Falls Nature Trail trailhead and walk a short stretch along the highway to reach the access path — pulling over directly at the falls isn't safe or legal in most spots along this section of road.

The short trail down is genuinely slippery, with muddy sections and exposed roots even in dry weather, and water levels can rise fast after rain upstream. Standard freshwater pool caution applies: avoid standing directly under the falls due to falling debris risk, and skip swimming entirely if the stream looks high, discolored, or fast-moving.
