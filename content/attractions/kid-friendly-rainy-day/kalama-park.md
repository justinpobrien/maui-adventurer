Kalama Park is one of South Maui's premier regional parks, spanning more than 36 acres along the Kihei coastline and offering enough variety of amenities to keep families with kids of different ages and interests occupied for a full afternoon.

The centerpiece for younger kids is a shaded playground — a meaningful feature given how strong South Maui sun can be at midday — with climbing structures, slides, and swings. Older kids and teens have options too: a skate park, an inline skating rink, tennis and basketball courts, and a multipurpose athletic field. The park also has twelve picnic pavilions, restrooms, showers, and direct beach access, making it easy to combine a playground visit with time in the water.

Free to visit and centrally located in Kihei, Kalama Park works well as a flexible, no-cost stop that can anchor a beach day or serve as a break between other South Maui activities — and its proximity to Cove Park's beginner surf break makes the two a natural pairing for families with older kids interested in trying surfing.
