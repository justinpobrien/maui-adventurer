Hamoa Beach is often cited as Hana's best all-around beach — a curved stretch of salt-and-pepper sand bordered by cliffs, with waves consistent enough for good bodysurfing and bodyboarding without being as intimidating as the surf at nearby Koki. Its setting, framed by palms and steep green slopes, has made it a longtime favorite even beyond Maui, going back to praise from famous 20th-century travel writers.

Facilities are simple: public restrooms, outdoor showers, and a foot-washing station near the parking area, but no lifeguard. Two access paths lead down to the sand — a set of stairs from the hotel shuttle drop-off point, and a smaller, steeper service road from the other end — both worth knowing about since the main path down is notably steep.

The real constraint here is parking. Only seven or eight parallel spots line Haneoo Road across from the beach access, and they go fast, particularly on weekends and during peak Road to Hana traffic. Early arrival isn't just a suggestion — it's close to a requirement if driving yourself.
