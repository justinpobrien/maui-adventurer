Mokapu Beach sits between Ulua Beach and Wailea Beach in the resort corridor of South Maui, and it's the low-key, family-oriented member of that trio. A manicured lawn, jogging path, and wooden racks for hanging dive gear give it a more park-like feel than its snorkel-focused neighbor next door.

The beach itself is calm and sandy, good for casual swimming rather than serious snorkeling, and it's genuinely family-friendly — Mokapu is one of the few beaches in this stretch where dogs are permitted. There's no lifeguard, and food and drinks aren't sold on the beach, but resorts and restaurants sit within an easy walk.

Parking is shared with Ulua Beach via two free lots that fill early in the morning. Because Mokapu connects directly to both Ulua and Wailea along the sand, it works best as part of a beach-hopping day rather than a single destination.
