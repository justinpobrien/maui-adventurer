Stand-up paddle boarding is one of the more approachable water activities on Maui, and the south shore around Kihei is generally considered the best place to learn thanks to consistently calm, wind-protected water compared to the more exposed windward coastline. Several operators run lessons ranging from complete-beginner instruction to guided coastal tours that combine paddling with snorkeling.

Action Sports Maui, based at 1900 South Kihei Road, offers 1.5-hour semi-private and private lessons with all gear included, and pricing shifts between summer and shoulder-season rates. Maui SUP Adventures runs private guided tours along a scenic stretch of coastline that includes underwater sea caves, lava cliffs, and coral reef, often bundling in a free digital photo package. Hawaiian Paddle Sports is another well-regarded name offering similar lesson and tour formats.

Morning sessions generally offer the calmest conditions before afternoon trade winds pick up, making early bookings the better choice for beginners in particular. Given the range of operators and formats, comparing a couple of options directly for current pricing and specific tour routes is worth doing before booking.
