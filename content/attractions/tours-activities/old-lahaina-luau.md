Old Lahaina Luau has become one of the most meaningful cultural anchors in Lahaina's post-fire recovery — it survived the 2023 wildfire and continued operating nightly from its location at the north end of the affected area, making it one of the first major cultural attractions to reopen after the disaster. For many visitors, booking a ticket here has taken on added significance as a tangible way to support the local community.

The experience includes a traditional Hawaiian buffet feast, an open bar, a lei greeting, cultural activities before the main show, and a full performance built around authentic Hawaiian music and hula rather than the more generalized Polynesian revue format used at some competing luaus.

Given both its reputation and its role in Lahaina's recovery, Old Lahaina Luau sells out days or weeks in advance, especially during summer travel season — booking well ahead of a Maui trip is essential rather than optional.
