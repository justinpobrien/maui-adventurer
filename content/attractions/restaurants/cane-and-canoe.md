Cane & Canoe, the signature restaurant at Montage Kapalua Bay, has rebranded to "The Restaurant at Kapalua Bay" — still operating and confirmed open, just under a new name that's worth knowing before making a reservation under the old branding. The kitchen's focus remains consistent: pristine local seafood, premium meats, and farm-fresh island produce.

A nightly three-course chef tasting menu, "Chef Purpura's Palate," offers a curated showcase of the kitchen's approach for guests wanting a guided experience rather than ordering a la carte. Breakfast runs 7 to 11am and dinner 5 to 10pm daily.

As a resort fine-dining destination within Montage Kapalua Bay, this sits at the top end of West Maui's dining scene, best suited to a special-occasion dinner with advance reservations rather than a casual drop-in.
