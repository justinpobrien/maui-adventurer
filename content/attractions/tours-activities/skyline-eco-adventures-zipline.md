Skyline Eco-Adventures holds a genuine claim to zipline history: founded in 2002 by father-and-son team Buck and Danny Borenat, it was the first zipline company established in the United States, and its original course remains at Historic Haleakala Ranch in Maui's upcountry.

The Haleakala course runs 5 lines with speeds up to 45 mph, crosses a long wooden swinging bridge, and includes the only pendulum zip (a zip that goes out and swings back) found anywhere on Maui — a distinctive feature compared to competing courses. Pricing runs roughly $158 to $167 per person depending on current rates and fees.

Requirements are straightforward: participants must be at least 8 years old and weigh between 60 and 260 pounds. The upcountry setting means cooler temperatures and views over ranch land toward the ocean, a different scenic backdrop than the coastal or rainforest ziplines found elsewhere on the island.
