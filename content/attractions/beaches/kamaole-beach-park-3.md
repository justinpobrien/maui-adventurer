Kamaole Beach Park III is the largest and liveliest of the three connected Kam beaches, with a generous lawn, a playground that keeps younger kids entertained off the sand, and the widest beach frontage of the trio. That popularity is a double-edged sword: it's the most reliably crowded of the three, especially on weekends, but the amenities and space make up for it.

Like Kam I and II, it has restrooms, freshwater showers, volleyball courts, barbecue stations, and lifeguard coverage roughly 8am-4:30pm. Kam III does have dedicated parking, unlike Kam II, though the Park Maui metered system applies here too.

A rocky point at the south end of the beach offers a bit of light snorkeling, giving Kam III slightly more variety than its purely sand-and-swim neighbors — a nice option if the kids want to build sandcastles while an adult sneaks off for a short snorkel.
