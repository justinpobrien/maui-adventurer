The Hawaiian Islands Humpback Whale National Marine Sanctuary Visitor Center in Kihei offers a free, low-key way to learn about the humpback whales that migrate to Hawaiian waters each winter, with interactive exhibits and displays covering whale biology, migration patterns, and the marine sanctuary's conservation work.

Admission is free, and the center is open Monday through Friday from 9:30am to 2:30pm, closed on weekends and federal holidays — worth building into a weekday itinerary specifically, since weekend visitors will find it closed. Located at 726 South Kihei Road, the beachfront setting itself offers views across the sanctuary waters toward Kaho'olawe, Lana'i, and the West Maui Mountains.

This pairs naturally with a McGregor Point or Papawai Point whale-watching stop during whale season (December through April), giving families both the educational context and the live viewing experience in the same general area of South Maui.
