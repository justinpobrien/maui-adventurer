Nick's Fishmarket Maui, located on the grounds of the Fairmont Kea Lani in Wailea, focuses on fresh Hawaiian seafood — opakapaka, mahi mahi, and ahi feature prominently — alongside filet mignon for guests wanting a non-seafood option. It's a longstanding fine-dining fixture in this part of South Maui, oriented toward a full sit-down dinner experience rather than casual dining.

Hours run dinner-only, roughly 5 to 9pm daily with last seating around 8:30pm. A daily happy hour from 4:30 to 6pm (except holidays) offers a more affordable window to experience the restaurant, useful for anyone wanting a taste without committing to full-price entrees.

Given the resort setting and fine-dining format, this is better suited to date nights or special occasions than a casual family dinner, though reservations can typically accommodate groups with advance notice.
