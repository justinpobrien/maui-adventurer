Casanova Italian Restaurant has anchored the center of Makawao — Maui's historic paniolo (cowboy) town — since 1989, serving authentic Italian food from a genuinely long-standing local institution rather than a newer transplant concept. The restaurant is known for its focaccia, house-made pastas, and wood-fired pizzas, alongside a full range of appetizers, salads, and seafood or meat entrees.

The business actually runs in two modes: a cafe and deli operating 8am to 2pm Monday through Saturday, and the full restaurant and bar taking over from 5 to 9pm daily. That split makes it worth checking which version will be operating depending on when a Makawao visit happens to fall.

Located at the main intersection of Makawao Avenue and Baldwin Avenue, it sits at the literal center of one of upcountry Maui's most charming small towns — worth noting this is a separate restaurant from Bistro Casanova in Kahului, despite the shared name and Italian focus.
