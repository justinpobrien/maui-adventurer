Haleakalā's summit sits above the cloud line, making sunrise here feel closer to a flight window than a hike. The park requires a separate sunrise reservation, booked apart from general admission.

Most visitors leave South or West Maui well before dawn to make the reserved window; the summit area has limited services, so it's worth fueling up and dressing warm beforehand.
