Horseback rides on Maui's North Shore ranch land — long known under the Mendes Ranch name — continue today under Circle M Ranch, which took over operations in May 2022. The ranch sits above the coast north of Wailuku, with trails winding through pastureland and offering sweeping Pacific Ocean views along the way.

Rides run Monday through Saturday with 9am and 12pm start times, typically lasting about two hours. A private ride option runs around $300 all-in, with group rides also available at different pricing — checking directly with the ranch or current booking platforms is worthwhile since the operator name change means some third-party sites may still reference outdated details.

The setting is genuinely working ranch land rather than a staged tourist attraction, giving the ride a different character from beach- or forest-based activities elsewhere on the island — a good option for visitors wanting a slower-paced, scenic outdoor activity.
