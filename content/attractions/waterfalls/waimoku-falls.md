Waimoku Falls is the dramatic payoff at the end of the Pipiwai Trail in Haleakala National Park's Kipahulu district — a 400-foot single-drop waterfall that holds the title of tallest on Maui. Reaching it requires the full trail experience: a 4-mile round trip past Makahiku Falls overlook, through a giant banyan grove, and into an otherworldly bamboo forest before the trees open up to reveal the falls itself.

The scale is the main draw here — this isn't a quick roadside waterfall but a genuine hiking destination, with the falls' height and the surrounding fern-draped cliff face making for one of the more striking natural sights on the island, especially after heavy rain when the flow is at its fullest.

Swimming at the base isn't recommended; falling rock and debris pose a real hazard directly under the falls, and rangers generally discourage lingering too close to the plunge pool. Access runs through the standard Haleakala National Park entrance fee ($30/vehicle, valid 3 days), with no separate fee for this specific trail.
