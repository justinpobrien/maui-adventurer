The Ohe'o Gulch overlook, in the Kipahulu district of Haleakala National Park about 15 minutes past Hana town, offers views over what's popularly called the Seven Sacred Pools — in reality closer to 20 pools cascading down Palikea Stream toward the ocean, rather than a literal seven.

Access falls under the same $30-per-vehicle Haleakala National Park entrance fee used at the summit, and the receipt is valid for three days at either location — so a summit visit earlier in the same trip covers Kipahulu entry too. Swimming in the pools is currently closed, but the overlook and trail remain open, giving visitors clear views of the pools and lower waterfalls without needing to get in the water.

This stop also serves as the trailhead for the Pipiwai Trail, the popular 4-mile round-trip hike through bamboo forest to 400-foot Waimoku Falls — making Ohe'o Gulch a natural anchor point for a longer stop near the end of the Road to Hana drive rather than a quick five-minute lookout.
