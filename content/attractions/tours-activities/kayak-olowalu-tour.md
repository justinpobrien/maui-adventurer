Olowalu, on Maui's west side between Lahaina and Ma'alaea, is home to one of the island's healthiest nearshore coral reefs and a reliable spot to encounter green sea turtles — making guided kayak tours here a popular combination of paddling and snorkeling rather than either activity alone.

Kayak Olowalu, the main dedicated operator, runs guided eco-tours of roughly 2.5 hours that combine kayaking with snorkeling stops near Turtle Town, with snorkel gear and light refreshments typically included. Other operators, including Maui Kayaks and Maui Adventure Tours Kayak Co., offer similar routes with options like clear-bottom kayaks that let non-snorkelers still see the reef and marine life below.

Pricing generally runs from about $109 for standard small-group tours up to $129 for specialty options like clear kayaks, with turtle sightings described as close to guaranteed by several operators given how consistently they're spotted in this area.
