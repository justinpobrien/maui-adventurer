Wailea Point offers a different kind of snorkeling experience than the easy, walk-right-in spots nearby — the reef close to shore is thin and unremarkable, meaning the real payoff requires committing to a swim of roughly 850 to 900 feet around the point toward the area facing the beachfront condos. That distance keeps casual snorkelers away and rewards those willing to make the trip with noticeably healthier coral and more varied fish life.

Visibility right at the beach entry tends to be poor, which can be discouraging for anyone expecting immediate results — the better water clarity and reef health show up only after rounding the point itself. This makes Wailea Point better suited to strong, confident swimmers than to beginners or families looking for an easy first snorkel.

Access is via the public parking area for Wailea Beach, with the point itself an extension of that same beach access rather than a separate trailhead. No lifeguard is on duty, and the swim distance itself is a meaningful safety consideration for anyone unsure of their open-water comfort level.
