Local Boys Shave Ice built a strong reputation since opening in 2003, known for one of the largest shave ice flavor selections in Hawaii — more than 40 flavors including li hing mui, lava flow, and free extras like Roselani ice cream, mochi, gummy bears, and azuki beans topped with Kauai cream. At its peak, the shop operated locations in both Kihei (Kihei Kalama Village) and on Front Street in Lahaina.

Current status is genuinely uncertain and worth flagging clearly: recent business listings show both the Kihei location and the Lahaina Front Street location marked as closed as of mid-2026. Whether this reflects a temporary closure, relocation, or permanent shutdown isn't clear from available sources, and the Lahaina closure may be tied to the 2023 wildfire given the Front Street address.

Given this uncertainty, this entry should not be treated as a confirmed, currently operating business. Anyone specifically seeking this shave ice experience should verify status directly (phone or current social media) before planning a stop, and Ululani's Hawaiian Shave Ice — with multiple confirmed-open Maui locations — is a reliable fallback in the meantime.
