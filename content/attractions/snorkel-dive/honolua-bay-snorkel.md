Honolua Bay is a shore-entry snorkel spot and Marine Life Conservation District on Maui's northwest coast, known for coral cover and calm water in summer months.

There's no sandy beach — entry is over a rocky shoreline that can be tricky with waves — but the payoff is a healthy reef close to shore. Conditions shift with the season, so it's worth checking swell forecasts before heading out.

Sources: Hawaii DLNR Division of Aquatic Resources
