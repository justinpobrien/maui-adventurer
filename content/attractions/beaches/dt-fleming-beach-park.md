D.T. Fleming Beach Park sits at the quieter northern end of West Maui's resort strip, offering a wider, more open stretch of sand than its bay-sheltered neighbors. The trade-off for that openness is more exposed surf — this beach sees real waves, which locals use for bodyboarding and beginner surfing, but which also means swimmers should pay closer attention to conditions than at Kapalua or Napili.

It's one of the better-equipped county parks on this coast: picnic tables, grills, restrooms, outdoor showers, wheelchair ramps, and a lifeguard station staffed daily from roughly 8am to 4:30pm. That lifeguard presence makes it a reasonable pick for families who want ocean access with a safety net nearby, though conditions can still turn rough enough to keep less confident swimmers closer to shore.

Parking follows the same pattern as its neighboring beaches — a small free lot at the end of the road fills early, while the main lot charges around $29 a day (roughly $34 with fees) via a QR-code payment system. Get there before mid-morning if you want a shot at free parking.
