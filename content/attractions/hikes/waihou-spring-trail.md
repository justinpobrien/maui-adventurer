Waihou Spring Trail offers a distinctly different Maui hiking experience — a cool, forested walk through pine and eucalyptus stands in the upcountry hills above Makawao, a world away from the beaches and coastal trails most visitors associate with the island. Total distance runs about 1 to 1.8 miles depending on the route taken, with a mild-to-moderate difficulty rating and roughly an hour to complete.

The hike starts gently through hilly forest before the final half-mile turns noticeably steeper, descending about 330 feet via switchbacks down into a gulch where Waihou Spring sits at the bottom. That descent — and the corresponding climb back out — is the main physical challenge on an otherwise easygoing trail.

Sitting at nearly 4,000 feet elevation, the trailhead (directly off Olinda Road, about 4.8 miles up from Makawao) tends to run cooler and mistier than coastal Maui, so a light layer is worth bringing even on a sunny beach day elsewhere on the island. No fee or permit is required.
