Waiheʻe Ridge climbs about 1,600 feet over roughly 4.5 miles round trip, following a knife-edge ridge with views back down the West Maui valleys to the ocean.

The first stretch is a paved access road before the trail narrows and starts climbing in earnest. On a clear morning the summit overlook takes in Waiheʻe Valley, the West Maui mountains, and the coast toward Kahului.

Sources: Hawaii DLNR Division of Forestry and Wildlife
