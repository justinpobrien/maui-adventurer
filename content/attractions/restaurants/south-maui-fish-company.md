South Maui Fish Company takes a genuinely fresh-catch approach — locally and sustainably caught fish, prepared same-day in poke bowls, fish tacos, and fish plates, with the option to buy fresh cleaned fillets to cook at home. Because the menu is built entirely around what's actually available that day, popular items like the mahi mahi tacos with homemade lime crema can and do sell out before closing.

The restaurant keeps split hours — lunch service from 11:30am to 2:30pm, then a break before dinner from 5 to 8pm — and is closed entirely on Sundays. That schedule rewards planning ahead rather than showing up whenever convenient.

For visitors who want to cook their own fresh Hawaiian fish back at a condo or vacation rental, the take-home fillet option is a notable feature that distinguishes this from most other casual seafood spots in Kihei, which serve prepared dishes only.
