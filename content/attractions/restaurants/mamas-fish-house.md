Mama's Fish House is Maui's best-known splurge restaurant — an oceanfront dining room in Paia serving fresh fish sourced from local day-boats, with a menu that names the specific fisherman and catch location for each dish. The setting, right on the sand with a classic Hawaiian beach-house feel, has made it a long-running favorite for anniversaries, proposals, and other special-occasion dinners.

Prices reflect the occasion: expect to spend $80 to $150 or more per person for dinner including a starter, entree, and drinks, with fish entrees alone ranging from $55 to $95 and cocktails in the $16-22 range. A table for two with appetizers, drinks, and dessert commonly runs $200 to $300 before tip.

The biggest planning consideration is the reservation window — most visitors need to book two to four months ahead, and peak season dates can fill up even further out, with the restaurant accepting bookings as much as 18 months in advance. Anyone hoping to eat here on a specific date during a Maui trip should call as early as the trip is booked, not wait until arrival.
