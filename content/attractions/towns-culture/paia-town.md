Pāʻia is a former sugar-plantation town turned windsurf and boutique hub at the start of the Road to Hana, with a walkable main street of shops, galleries, and cafes.

It's a practical last stop for gas, snacks, and cash before the drive east, but also a pleasant destination on its own for an easy morning of browsing and coffee.

Sources: Hawaii Visitors and Convention Bureau
