Amigo's brings a genuinely broad Mexican menu to Kihei, covering familiar staples — fajitas, tacos, guacamole, burgers — alongside more traditional dishes like menudo, lengua (tongue), caldo de res, and pozole that go beyond the typical tourist-facing Mexican restaurant menu. Tamales and churros round out the offering for both savory and sweet cravings.

Open 8am to 9pm seven days a week, the restaurant runs two distinct happy hour windows: a standard 3-6pm slot with the usual drink and appetizer discounts, plus an unusual breakfast happy hour from 8 to 11am featuring discounted tacos, margaritas, ceviche, and quesadillas — a solid option for anyone wanting a lighter, earlier meal with a Mexican flavor profile.

Full delivery, takeout, and dine-in service is available, making it a flexible choice whether eating in or bringing food back to a nearby condo or vacation rental.
