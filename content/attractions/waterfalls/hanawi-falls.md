Hanawi Falls is a Hana Highway landmark best experienced without stopping — the upper falls are clearly visible from the historic 1926 bridge at mile marker 24, no hiking or even parking required, just a well-timed glance (or passenger photo) while crossing. A consistent stream feeds the falls year-round, making it one of the more dependable waterfalls along the route compared to seasonal cascades that thin out during dry spells.

A more dramatic 200-foot lower falls exists further downstream but isn't accessible to visitors, sitting on private land with genuine safety concerns around the surrounding terrain. Even setting aside the private land, the descent to any closer vantage point is steep, slippery, and prone to rockslides and flash floods — this isn't a spot to go looking for an off-trail path.

Because there's no safe place to pull over and park directly at the falls, the practical approach is simple: slow down on the bridge, enjoy the view, and let a passenger snap photos rather than stopping the car. Swimming isn't recommended here under any circumstances, given the currents and inaccessible terrain around the falls.
