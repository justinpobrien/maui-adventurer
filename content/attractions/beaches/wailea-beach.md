Wailea Beach is the wide, manicured stretch of sand fronting Wailea's resort row, and it delivers exactly what that setting promises: calm, clear water, soft sand, and a paved coastal path that makes it easy to wander between beaches without dealing with traffic. It's a strong pick for swimming and easy floating rather than a top snorkeling destination.

Public access is straightforward — a free lot off Wailea Alanui Drive leads to a beach walkway between the resorts, and restrooms, outdoor showers, and shaded areas are available at the public access point. Despite the resort backdrop, there's no lifeguard here, so the calm appearance shouldn't fully substitute for keeping watch, especially with young children.

The real advantage of Wailea Beach is the connecting path: it links north to Mokapu and Ulua and south toward Polo Beach, so a single parking spot can turn into a multi-beach afternoon without moving the car.
