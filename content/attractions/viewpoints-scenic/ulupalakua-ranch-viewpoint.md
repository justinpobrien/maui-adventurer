Ulupalakua Ranch, an 11,000-acre working cattle ranch on Haleakala's southwestern slopes, offers one of Maui's most underrated scenic overlooks — a wide view across the Central Valley to the West Maui Mountains, with the Pacific stretching out beyond and Molokini, Lanai, and Kaho'olawe visible on clear days.

The ranch is also home to MauiWine, Hawaii's oldest winery and the island's only one, with a tasting room set inside the historic King's Cottage, built in the 1870s to host King Kalakaua. The 23-acre vineyard produces both traditional grape wines (Syrah, Malbec, Grenache, Viognier, Chenin Blanc, Gewürztraminer) and the winery's better-known pineapple wines. Tastings run Tuesday through Sunday, 11am to 5pm, with picnic tables available on the lawn among century-old trees.

Located along the Kula Highway well past the more commonly visited upcountry stops, Ulupalakua rewards visitors willing to drive a bit further past Kula — a quieter, working-ranch counterpoint to Maui's busier tourist attractions, combining genuine scenery with a low-key wine tasting stop.
