Pacific Whale Foundation runs whale watching tours as part of its broader marine conservation nonprofit mission, meaning proceeds support ongoing research alongside the tourist experience. The standard tour is a 2-hour narrated cruise led by certified marine naturalists who explain humpback whale behavior in real time, including listening to whale songs through onboard hydrophones — a level of educational depth that sets it apart from purely recreational whale-watch operators.

Pricing runs from about $88 per person for the classic tour up to $138 for options that include lunch, with online direct booking through the foundation's own site typically saving about 20% versus third-party platforms.

The tour is strictly seasonal, running roughly November 1 through April 15 with peak activity from mid-January through March, tracking the humpback whales' actual migration to Hawaiian waters. Check-in happens 45 minutes before departure at the Pacific Whale Foundation Ocean Store in Ma'alaea Harbor Shops, next to the Maui Ocean Center.
