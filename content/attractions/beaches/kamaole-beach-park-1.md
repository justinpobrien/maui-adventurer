Kamaole Beach Park I is the first of the three connected "Kam" beaches lining South Kihei Road, and it sets the tone for the whole trio: golden sand, generally calm morning water, and a full slate of county facilities including restrooms, showers, volleyball courts, and barbecue stations. A lifeguard is stationed here during daylight hours, making it a dependable choice for families.

Parking is spread across the main lot facing the beach, on-street spots, and an overflow lot across the road — though all of it now runs through the Park Maui metered parking program, so visitors should expect to pay (Hawaii residents with a valid state driver's license park free).

Kam I tends to be the quietest of the three Kamaole parks, without the crowds that build up at Kam III down the road, making it a solid pick for a lower-key beach morning before the rest of Kihei wakes up.
