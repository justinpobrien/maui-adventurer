Coconut's Fish Cafe has built a loyal following in Kihei around simple, well-executed wild-caught fish dishes — fish tacos, fish and chips, and fish "burgers" (sandwiches) that lean on fresh, island-inspired preparation rather than heavy sauces or gimmicks. Kalua pork, fresh island salads, and vegetarian-friendly options round out a menu that keeps things approachable for groups with different tastes.

Two locations operate in Kihei — the original at Azeka Shopping Center (1279 S Kihei Rd) and a second at Kamaole Plaza (2463 S Kihei Rd) — both open daily from 11am to 9pm. This gives visitors staying anywhere along the Kihei strip a reasonably convenient option regardless of exactly where they're based.

A twice-daily happy hour (2:30-4:30pm and again 7:30-8:30pm) offers deals like discounted shrimp, making off-peak dining a genuine value opportunity rather than just an empty-restaurant consolation.
