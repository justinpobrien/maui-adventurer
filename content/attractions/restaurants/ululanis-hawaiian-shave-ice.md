Ululani's Hawaiian Shave Ice is one of Maui's most reliable dessert stops, with a consistent presence across multiple islands and, as of 2026, four active locations spread across central and south Maui: Kihei (61 S Kihei Rd, 10:30am-6:30pm), Kahului (333 Dairy Rd, 10:30am-6pm), Wailuku (50 Maui Lani Parkway, 10:30am-6pm), and a newer counter inside the Maui Ocean Center at Ma'alaea (10am-4pm).

The shave ice itself is finely textured rather than icy and crunchy, a hallmark that's built the brand's reputation, typically served with a wide range of syrup flavors and options like mochi, ice cream, or sweet azuki beans mixed in or on top.

Worth noting for anyone using older guides: the original Lahaina Marketplace location on Front Street remains closed, a casualty of the 2023 Lahaina wildfire. The four locations listed above are the current, confirmed-operating spots as of 2026.
