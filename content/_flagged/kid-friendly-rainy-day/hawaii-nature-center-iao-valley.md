The Hawaii Nature Center's former Iao Valley facility is not currently open to the general public and should not be treated as a drop-in attraction. Following significant flood damage and pandemic-era closures, the organization sold its Iao Valley property in 2022 and transitioned to a mobile programming model instead of operating from a single fixed location.

Today, Hawaii Nature Center runs school programs and Nature Adventure Camps at various sites across Maui — including at Kealia Pond National Wildlife Refuge — rather than welcoming general visitors to an Iao Valley building. Families interested in the organization's educational programming should check directly with hawaiinaturecenter.org for current camp locations, schedules, and enrollment rather than planning a visit to the old Iao Valley address.

This entry is included primarily as a flag: older travel guides and some third-party listings may still reference a walk-in nature center in Iao Valley, but that facility no longer operates in that form.
