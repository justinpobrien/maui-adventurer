A Saigon Cafe has long been one of Wailuku's most reliable Vietnamese restaurants, offering an extensive menu that ranges well beyond the usual pho-and-spring-rolls formula to include hot pot, chow mein, vermicelli bowls, seafood, steaks, and a solid range of vegetarian dishes. The sheer menu length makes it a good option for groups with different cravings.

As of early 2026, the restaurant is confirmed open and operating normally, with daily hours from 11am to 8:30pm at its longtime Main Street location. This is a straightforward sit-down restaurant rather than counter service, with a full menu spanning appetizers through entrees.

Given its central Wailuku location, it works well as a stop after visiting Iao Valley or exploring the historic town, offering a different flavor profile than the plate-lunch and Hawaiian-regional spots more common elsewhere in central Maui.
