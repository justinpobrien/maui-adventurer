Keawakapu Beach is the beach South Maui regulars quietly point newcomers toward — a long stretch of soft sand with fewer crowds than the Kamaole parks up the road, calm water, and a gentle slope that makes entry easy for kids and less confident swimmers. It's especially known for sunset viewing, with visitors regularly describing the sky here as fading from gold through orange into lavender.

Access is split across three separate free lots: one near the Mana Kai Maui at the north end, one at the corner of South Kihei Road and Kilohana Drive in the middle, and one where South Kihei Road dead-ends to the south. Picking the right lot for the stretch of beach you want saves a longer walk.

There's no lifeguard, but conditions are generally mild, particularly through summer, making Keawakapu a solid choice for families who want a quieter alternative to the more heavily trafficked Kihei beaches.
