Wai'anapanapa State Park's coastal overlook is one of the most photographed stops along the Road to Hana, taking in the black sand beach cove, a natural sea arch, blowholes, and lava tube caves along a dramatically rugged stretch of coastline near Hana town.

Access now runs strictly through an advance reservation system: reservations open 30 days ahead and release daily at midnight Hawai'i time, with visitors choosing either a morning (7-10am) or afternoon (12:30-3pm) entry window. Same-day reservations aren't available, and arriving more than 30 minutes past your window's start can mean being denied entry — so this needs to be planned into a Road to Hana itinerary well ahead of the actual drive, not decided on the fly.

Fees run $5 per person plus $10 per vehicle for non-residents. Note that camper van camping at the park is temporarily paused from July 2026 through March 2027 while a new comfort station is built, though this doesn't affect day-use lookout access.
