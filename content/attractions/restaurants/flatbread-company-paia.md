Flatbread Company brings a Vermont-born, organic-focused wood-fired pizza concept to Paia's Hana Highway strip, and it's become one of the town's most consistently busy dinner spots as a result. The menu keeps things simple — a handful of organic salads alongside a full lineup of flatbreads, split between sauce-free and cauldron-tomato-sauce versions.

The signature order is the Mopsy's Kalua Pork flatbread: kiawe-smoked free-range pork shoulder, homemade organic mango BBQ sauce, red onions, Maui pineapple, Hawaiian goat cheese, mozzarella, parmesan, and a garlic-herb oil finish — a genuinely distinctive combination that draws repeat visitors.

Open daily 11am to 9pm, the restaurant regularly draws a wait at the door in the evenings; this is a sign of popularity rather than a problem, but anyone in a hurry should call in a to-go order or use online ordering instead of waiting for a table.
