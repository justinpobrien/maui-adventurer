Waiehu Beach Park is one of the least touristed beaches on Maui's central coast, favored by locals precisely because it sees a fraction of the traffic that hits Kanaha or Baldwin nearby. It's a straightforward park — picnic tables and benches set along a quieter stretch of shoreline — without the crowds, and without much in the way of built-out amenities either.

There are no restrooms or shower facilities on site, so this isn't a beach to plan a long day around unless you're prepared to go without. What it offers instead is a low-key, uncrowded alternative for a swim or a picnic, particularly appealing to visitors who've already hit the busier north shore spots and want something calmer.

Access is via free street parking along Lower Waiehu Beach Road, with a small lot at the northern end. It sits near the Waiehu Municipal Golf Course, making it a convenient stop for golfers looking to cool off after a round.
