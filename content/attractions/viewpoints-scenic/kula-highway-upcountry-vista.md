The Kula Highway (Route 37) offers a different kind of Maui scenic drive from the famous coastal routes: a cooler-elevation loop through the island's upcountry farm country, passing through Makawao, Kula, Ulupalakua, and Pukalani at elevations around 2,500 feet and higher.

Rather than ocean cliffs and waterfalls, the appeal here is rolling pastureland, small farms, gardens, and small-town charm, plus genuinely expansive views back down toward the coast. The Ulupalakua Scenic Overlook along this route is frequently cited as one of Maui's best vista points and among its most overlooked, with sightlines across the Central Valley to the West Maui Mountains and, on clear days, out to neighboring islands.

Because it draws far less tourist traffic than the Road to Hana, this drive suits visitors who want scenery without crowds or a full-day commitment — it pairs naturally with stops at the Ali'i Kula Lavender Farm, Surfing Goat Dairy, or MauiWine at Ulupalakua Ranch for a relaxed upcountry half-day.
