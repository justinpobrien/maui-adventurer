Alelele Falls is an 80-foot waterfall in the Kipahulu district of Haleakala National Park that, until recently, drew a small trickle of visitors willing to follow Alelele Stream upstream from the white bridge near mile marker 39. That's no longer an option: the trail is currently closed indefinitely due to severe erosion and landslide risk, according to current park information, superseding older blog posts and trip reports that describe it as a short, easy detour.

Given that closure, this entry exists mainly to flag the status clearly rather than to recommend a visit. Anyone who encounters older directions to Alelele Falls online should treat them as outdated — the erosion and landslide concerns are a safety matter, not a bureaucratic technicality, and the closure should be respected rather than worked around.

If the trail reopens in the future, access would fall under Haleakala National Park's standard entrance fee, with no separate permit historically required. Until then, this is a waterfall to skip on any current Road to Hana itinerary.
