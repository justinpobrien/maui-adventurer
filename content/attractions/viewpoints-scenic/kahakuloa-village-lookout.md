A pullout above Kahakuloa Village frames one of the most remote-feeling views on Maui — a small settlement wedged into a steep green valley against the north shore.

Getting here means driving the narrow, cliff-hugging back side of the West Maui loop road, which is part of the appeal but not for every driver or rental car. A couple of informal roadside stands along the way sell banana bread and fruit.
