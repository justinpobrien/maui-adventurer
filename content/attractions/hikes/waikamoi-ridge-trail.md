Waikamoi Ridge Trail is the classic Road to Hana leg-stretch — a short, free, no-permit loop through dense rainforest just off Highway 360 near mile marker 9.5. At 0.8 miles for the basic loop (with an optional extension to about 1.5 miles), it's an easy add-on rather than a destination hike, but it delivers a genuine dose of Maui's interior forest in under an hour.

The terrain is gentle, with around 200-225 feet of elevation gain, rated easy by Hawaii's Department of Land and Natural Resources. Expect grasslands giving way to forest, eucalyptus and native trees overhead, and ground that's often muddy or rough underfoot after rain — a sign posted at the trailhead reads "Ridge trail, not for the timid," which overstates the actual difficulty but hints at the uneven footing.

A small picnic area near the trailhead makes this a natural stop for a quick snack break mid-drive. No fee, no permit, and no reservation are required — just pull off at the marked lot and go.
