Kimo's was a longtime fixture on Lahaina's Front Street before being lost in the devastating 2023 wildfire, and it remains closed as of this writing. There is genuine, recent movement toward its return, however: as of July 2026, T S Restaurants (the group behind Kimo's) signed a letter of intent to rebuild the restaurant on Front Street, in a restored historic building known as the Chan Wa building.

The timing is notable — Lahaina's Front Street itself is scheduled to reopen to the public around August 1, 2026, and Kimo's rebuild appears timed to align with that broader reopening. This makes Kimo's one of the more promising "coming soon" stories in Lahaina's ongoing recovery, though as of now it remains unconfirmed and unopened.

Given the current status, this entry should be treated as informational rather than an active recommendation. Anyone specifically interested in dining at Kimo's should check the restaurant's official site for a confirmed reopening date before planning a Maui trip around it.
