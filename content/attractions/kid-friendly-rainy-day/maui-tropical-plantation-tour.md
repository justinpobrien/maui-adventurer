A narrated tram winds through working farmland outside Wailuku, covering pineapple, cacao, and other tropical crops grown on the plantation grounds.

The pace is easy and the covered tram makes it workable even in light rain, with an on-site restaurant and shops for before or after. It's a low-key half-day option that doesn't require much advance planning.
