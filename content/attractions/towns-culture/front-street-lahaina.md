Front Street was the commercial and historic heart of Lahaina before the 2023 wildfire destroyed most of the buildings along it. As of this writing, the street is on the verge of a major reopening: the stretch between Papalaua and Shaw Streets reopens to regular vehicle traffic on August 1, 2026, ending three years of restricted or closed access along that section.

This reopening is genuinely significant but partial. The section of Front Street most visitors remember — packed with art galleries, oceanview restaurants, and historic storefronts — remains behind construction fencing as recovery work continues, and adjacent streets like Market Street and Papelekane Street stay closed while the state completes dredging work at Lahaina Harbor.

For visitors, this means Front Street in 2026 is best approached as a place to witness Lahaina's ongoing recovery rather than a fully restored destination — some businesses are reopening and returning (including Kimo's and the 'Ulu o Lele Marketplace), but the full pre-fire experience is still years away by most estimates.
