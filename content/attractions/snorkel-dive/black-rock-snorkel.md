The base of Pu'u Keka'a — Black Rock — is one of the easiest, most reliable snorkeling spots on Maui, requiring no boat, no long swim, and no advanced skill to enjoy. The volcanic promontory creates a natural reef edge close to shore, and green sea turtles are a common sight gliding through alongside parrotfish, needlefish, and the occasional eagle ray.

Because access is via the public beach between the Sheraton Maui and Ka'anapali Beach Hotel, this spot combines genuine reef variety with resort-level convenience — restrooms, showers, and food and drink all within easy walking distance. That convenience comes with crowds, particularly by mid-morning, so early arrival pays off both for parking and for having the water to yourself before tour groups and hotel guests arrive in force.

Conditions are generally calm and forgiving, making this one of the better beginner and family snorkel spots in West Maui, though as with any ungated beach, no lifeguard is specifically posted at the water's edge here.
