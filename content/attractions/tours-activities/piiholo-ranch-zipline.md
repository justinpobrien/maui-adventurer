Piiholo Ranch Zipline, located above Makawao in Maui's upcountry, has historically offered a varied lineup: a side-by-side zip course with 5 lines (including the longest side-by-side zipline on Maui at over half a mile), a separate treetop course soaring through the Kahakapao Valley's eucalyptus forest with stops at a "Pirate Ship Tree House," hiking tours, and a combined zip-and-waterfall-hike package.

There's a genuine status question worth flagging directly: a current Yelp business listing shows this location marked as closed as of mid-2026, while several booking platforms (Tripster, Groupon, Maui Tickets for Less) continue to list active tour options and pricing. This kind of conflict often indicates either a temporary closure not yet reflected everywhere, or an outdated closure notice — either way, confirming directly with the ranch before booking or planning a visit is the responsible move.

If operating, pricing has historically started around $135 for a roughly three-hour, six-line canopy tour, with occasional promotions offering free zips for kids on select courses.
