Merriman's Kapalua is chef Peter Merriman's Maui outpost, built around a genuinely farm-to-table philosophy — roughly 90% of the menu is locally sourced from Hawaii's own farmers and fishermen, translating into dishes that change with what's actually in season rather than a fixed, generic menu.

The restaurant pairs its food program with an oceanfront setting well-suited to sunset dining, backed by an extensive wine list, a specialty cocktail menu, and nightly live entertainment. Dinner is served daily from 4 to 8:30pm, with happy hour running 3-5pm at the Oceanfront Point and 4-5pm at the main bar.

For a more relaxed, walk-in option, "The Point" — a casual oceanside dining area — opens daily at 3pm (weather permitting) and doesn't require reservations, making it a good fallback if the main dining room is booked or a full dinner commitment isn't the goal.
