Kula Bistro brings genuine Italian cooking to Maui's upcountry, serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner from a cozy space in Kula. Breakfast (7:30-10:30am daily except Tuesday) covers benedicts, frittatas, and griddle items alongside local favorites, while lunch and dinner (11am-8pm) shift to pastas, pizzas, and paninis — shrimp scampi and Maryland crab cake paninis with sweet Thai chili aioli are among the standout orders.

Two operational quirks are worth knowing before visiting: the restaurant takes walk-ins only, with no reservation system, and it operates on a BYOB basis — wine and beer aren't sold on-site but can be picked up at the general store directly across the street.

Located in Kula's cooler upcountry elevation, it makes a good stop when combined with a Haleakala sunrise or sunset trip, or simply as a change of pace from Maui's coastal dining scene.
