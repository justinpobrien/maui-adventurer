A compact, locally-run market with a rotating set of fruit stands, baked goods, and a couple of coffee carts — a good low-key stop before a beach morning.

It's open mornings only in a small lot in Kihei, so it works best as an early errand rather than a planned destination — pair it with a nearby beach for the rest of the morning.
