Kihei Caffe has built a reputation as one of Maui's most consistently praised breakfast spots, repeatedly voted best breakfast in the area for multiple years running. The menu covers more than 40 breakfast options — omelets, pancakes, French toast, biscuits and gravy, and Hawaiian classics like loco moco and chorizo and eggs — served all day rather than cut off at a specific hour.

Lunch options round out the later hours with sandwiches, burgers, salads, and wraps, giving the restaurant flexibility beyond a strict breakfast-only identity. It's a quick-service, no-frills operation rather than a sit-down experience with table service throughout, which keeps turnover fast even when it's busy.

Open daily from 6am to 2pm, it's a solid early stop before a beach day, snorkeling trip, or long drive — including as a pre-dawn fueling stop for anyone heading up to Haleakala for sunrise.
