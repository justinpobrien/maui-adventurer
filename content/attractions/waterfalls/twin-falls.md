Twin Falls earns its spot on both the waterfalls and hikes lists because it delivers on both fronts equally: the trails to reach it are short and easy, and the payoff is a set of genuinely swimmable pools right at the base of the falls — a rarer combination on the Road to Hana than it might seem, since many of the route's most dramatic waterfalls (Waimoku, Hanawi) explicitly discourage or prohibit swimming.

As the first major waterfall stop for most people driving from Kahului, Twin Falls tends to set expectations for the rest of the drive — expect a working farm parking operation (private, not a state park), a $10 per car fee covering trail upkeep, and pools ranging from shallow wading depth to deep enough for a proper swim.

Because the property is privately managed, it comes with its own access rules: closed to non-Hawaii residents on the first Saturday of each month, and subject to closure after heavy rain. Checking the official Twin Falls Maui site before setting out is the simplest way to avoid a wasted stop.
