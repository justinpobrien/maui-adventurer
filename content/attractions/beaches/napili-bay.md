Napili Bay is a near-perfect crescent of sand tucked between two low points of lava rock, and it's earned a loyal following among people who'd rather stay in a quiet condo than a big resort. The bay's gentle, sandy-bottomed water and rocky fringes make it a reliable spot for both casual swimming and beginner snorkeling, with sea turtles a frequent sight along the rockier southern edge.

The catch is access. There's no public parking lot here — just a scattering of street spots on Hui Drive and Napili Place that disappear well before most people finish breakfast. Locals and repeat visitors treat sunrise arrival as the price of admission; anyone rolling up mid-morning should have a backup plan.

Conditions shift with the season. Summer (roughly May through September) is calm and clear, ideal for families and inexperienced swimmers. Winter swell changes the character of the beach considerably, producing a shore break strong enough to knock people down — worth respecting given there's no lifeguard on duty at any time of year.
