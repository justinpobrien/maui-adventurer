Whalers Village sits directly in the middle of Ka'anapali Beach, functioning as the commercial hub for the string of resorts lining this stretch of West Maui coastline. More than 90 stores and restaurants cover everything from clothing and jewelry to art, souvenirs, and even real estate offices, making it a genuine one-stop shopping destination rather than a small boutique strip.

Worth noting clearly: Whalers Village is in Ka'anapali, a distinct area from Lahaina town, and was unaffected by the 2023 wildfire that devastated Lahaina's historic core. It remains fully open and operating on its normal schedule, a useful fact for visitors uncertain about what's currently accessible in West Maui.

Open daily from 9:30am to 10pm, its central location makes it walkable from most of the major nearby resorts — the Hyatt, Marriott, Kaanapali Alii, Westin, Whaler, Kaanapali Beach Hotel, and Sheraton are all within easy reach on foot.
