Kapalua Ziplines (also referred to as West Maui Mountain Zipline) bills itself as Maui's only all-dual zipline course, letting riders zip side-by-side rather than one at a time — a genuine differentiator from single-line competitors. The course spans about 2 miles of cable across lines ranging from 500 to 2,100 feet, set above a tropical rainforest in the West Maui Mountains.

A highlight beyond the ziplining itself is a crossing of the longest suspension bridge in Hawaii, and ATVs shuttle guests between zipline stations through the rainforest, adding a bit of off-road adventure to the overall experience. Pricing for the 6-line tour runs roughly $220 to $255 per person depending on current promotions and fees.

Requirements: participants must be at least 10 years old, with adults required to accompany anyone between 10 and 17, and weight limits run 60 to 250 pounds. Given its popularity, the course often sells out a week or more in advance during summer and holiday periods, making early booking a real practical consideration rather than just good advice.
