Bistro Casanova offers a genuinely elevated dining option in an area of Maui — Kahului — better known for casual, quick-service spots than sit-down Italian cuisine. The menu covers homemade ravioli, wild mushroom linguine, angel hair checca, paella, cioppino, and steaks prepared four different ways, plus 10-inch pizzette flatbreads for lighter orders.

Hours split across several service periods: weekday-only lunch from 11am to 2pm, tapas from 4 to 9pm, happy hour 4 to 6pm, and dinner served 5 to 9pm every day of the week. That structure makes it flexible for both a full sit-down dinner and a lighter tapas-and-drinks stop.

Located on the ground floor of an office building about a mile from Kahului Airport, it's a practical option for a nicer meal near the airport — whether celebrating the start of a trip or making the most of a last evening on Maui before an early flight out.
