A Molokini snorkel cruise is the standard way most visitors experience the partially submerged volcanic crater, since it's accessible only by boat. Multiple well-established operators run daily trips from Ma'alaea Harbor, generally in the $150 to $190 per adult range, with children (ages 3-12) typically getting $30-45 discounts and some operators offering free admission for very young kids.

Departure times vary by operator: Malolo leaves earliest at 6:30am, Four Winds III checks in at 7am for a 7:30am departure, and Pride of Maui departs at 8am from the North Dock. Earlier departures generally correspond to calmer water and clearer visibility before wind picks up later in the morning.

Most packages include breakfast, lunch, snorkel gear, basic instruction, and flotation devices, making the ticket price effectively all-inclusive for a half-day outing. With Molokini being a Marine Life Conservation District, all operators follow no-fishing, no-anchoring-on-coral rules that have helped keep the site's reef in strong condition.
