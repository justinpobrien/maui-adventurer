The Maui Gold Pineapple Tour offers a genuinely unique agricultural experience: a guided visit to Hawai'i's last remaining working pineapple farm, based in Haliimaile in Maui's upcountry. The tour covers the growing and harvesting process for Maui Gold pineapple, a proprietary low-acid variety developed specifically for Maui's soil and climate, and includes a tasting plus a free whole pineapple to take home.

Pricing varies noticeably across booking platforms — standard listings run $145 for ages 13+ and $125 for children 3-12, while some third-party sites show discounted rates closer to $87-99 for adults — so comparing current listings directly before booking is worthwhile.

As of May 2026, the operator added a new Farm Tour and Lunch Experience package, pairing the standard guided walking tour with a catered meal from local caterer 'Ohana Island Grindz, giving visitors a more complete half-day option beyond the basic tour-and-tasting format. Reservations can be made at mauipineappletour.com or by phone.
