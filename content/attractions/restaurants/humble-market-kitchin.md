Humble Market Kitchin, from James Beard Award-winning chef Roy Yamaguchi, sits at the Wailea Beach Resort and draws directly on Yamaguchi's own family history on Maui's sugarcane plantations. The menu translates that plantation-era heritage into refined dishes like Maui beef lumpia, kalbi-glazed short ribs, potato-crusted opakapaka, and a melting hot chocolate souffle — familiar comfort flavors elevated through fine-dining technique.

Service runs breakfast (6:30-10:30am) and dinner (5-8:30pm) daily, with no lunch service in between. A daily happy hour from 5 to 6pm offers a lower-commitment way to try the restaurant, with discounts on draft beer, cocktails, wine, and appetizers before the full dinner menu kicks in.

As a resort restaurant carrying a well-known chef's name, reservations are worth securing in advance, particularly for dinner during Maui's busier travel seasons.
