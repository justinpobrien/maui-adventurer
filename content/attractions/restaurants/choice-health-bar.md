Choice Health Bar has rebuilt after a genuine loss — the original flagship location in Lahaina was destroyed in the 2023 Maui wildfires, and the business relocated to the Wailea Village Shopping Center on the south side of the island. Anyone working from older travel guides listing a Lahaina address should update to the current Kihei/Wailea location before planning a visit.

The menu focuses on organic, locally sourced wellness food: no-sugar-added acai bowls, protein-packed smoothies, cold-pressed juice and cleanses, avocado toast, kale salads, soups, and a range of plant-based entrees. It's a solid counterpoint to Maui's fried-fish and plate-lunch-heavy casual dining scene for anyone wanting a lighter option.

Open daily from 7am to 3pm, it works best as a morning or early-afternoon stop rather than a dinner destination, fitting naturally into a beach-day or hiking-day itinerary before or after the main activity.
