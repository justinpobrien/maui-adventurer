Kula Lodge Restaurant was, before August 2025, a well-regarded fine-dining destination in Maui's upcountry, known for dishes like squash ravioli, market-price fresh catch, and Maui coffee-dusted ribeye alongside sweeping views over the island. That restaurant building was destroyed by fire in August 2025, and the main dining room remains closed as of this writing — a separate incident from the 2023 Lahaina wildfires, worth distinguishing clearly.

In the meantime, the same chef has set up a food truck near the original site, serving some of the same dishes from the pre-fire menu, giving visitors a way to still experience a version of the Kula Lodge dining concept. The adjacent Kula Lodge Marketplace was unaffected by the fire and continues operating normally.

Anyone planning a Maui visit around Kula Lodge dining should treat the original restaurant as closed and check current status (official site or social media) for updates on rebuilding progress, while the food truck and marketplace remain the active options in the meantime.
