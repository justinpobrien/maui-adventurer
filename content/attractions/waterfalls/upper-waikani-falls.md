Upper Waikani Falls, nicknamed Three Bears for its three distinct cascades — playfully dubbed Papa, Mama, and Baby Bear — is one of the more photogenic stops between mile markers 19 and 20 on the Hana Highway. Each tier drops down a cliff face into a shared, crystal-clear pool below, visible right from the roadside bridge without any real hiking required.

The swimming hole at the base is a popular draw, and the water is deep enough for a proper dip, but the pool bottom is scattered with submerged rocks that make cliff jumping or diving a genuinely bad idea despite how inviting the setup looks from above. Getting down to the pool itself involves picking a careful path over uneven, rocky terrain.

Parking is the trickiest part of visiting: the small lot right at the falls has been closed, and parking directly at the bridge isn't permitted. The practical workaround is parking about a tenth of a mile past the falls and walking back along the narrow road shoulder — arriving early in the morning helps avoid both crowding and the awkward roadside walk during heavier traffic.
