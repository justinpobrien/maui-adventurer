Oneuli — "dark sands" in Hawaiian — is one of only two black sand beaches on Maui, tucked into the Makena caldera at the base of Pu'u Ola'i's north side. The sand here is actually a blend of three colors: black, white, and a reddish tint from the surrounding volcanic terrain, giving it a noticeably different look from the golden beaches nearby.

Coral reef sits just past the water's entry, making Oneuli a decent, low-key spot for snorkeling and diving without the crowds of Ulua or Molokini charters. That said, it comes with zero infrastructure — no restrooms, no lifeguard, no shade — so this is a bring-everything-you-need kind of stop.

Finding it takes a little effort: there's no beach signage from the highway, and the access road is unmarked beyond an unassuming yellow gate. Typing "Oneuli Beach Access" into Google Maps and following the dirt road to the small parking area is the most reliable way in.
