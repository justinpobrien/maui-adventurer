Milagros Food Co brings island-inflected Tex-Mex to the corner of Baldwin Avenue and Hana Highway in Paia, pairing fresh local fish with Southwestern staples like enchiladas, tacos, burritos, and nachos. The margarita bar and local draft beer selection give it a lively, casual-dinner energy that fits well with Paia's easygoing North Shore vibe.

Standout dishes according to regulars include shrimp enchiladas in red chile sauce, soft pork tacos, generously stuffed burritos, and mahi mahi fish tacos — a solid mix of classic Mexican preparations executed with fresh, local seafood. Outdoor seating is available for both lunch and dinner, adding to the relaxed atmosphere.

Open daily 11am to 9pm (kitchen open until 10pm), with happy hour running 3 to 6pm daily for discounted drinks and food — useful to know if timing a visit around a deal rather than full price.
