The Maui Arts & Cultural Center is the island's primary large-scale performance venue, anchored by the Castle Theater and hosting a genuinely wide range of programming — music, theater, dance, and comedy acts spanning both local Maui talent and touring national performers. It functions as Maui's answer to a proper concert hall and cultural center rather than a single-purpose theater.

Alongside the main performance spaces, an on-site gallery showcases visual art on its own separate schedule (Wednesday through Saturday, 10am to 4pm), giving visitors a reason to stop by even without a ticketed show planned for that day.

Office hours run Monday through Friday, 9am to 5pm, with the box office handling phone inquiries Tuesday through Friday, 10am to 4pm. As with most performance venues, checking the current event calendar at mauiarts.org/events before visiting is essential since the specific programming changes constantly.
