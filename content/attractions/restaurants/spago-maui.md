Spago Maui, Wolfgang Puck's outpost at the Four Seasons Resort Maui in Wailea, remains one of the island's top fine-dining destinations, confirmed open and operating as of July 2026. The menu blends Puck's established signature dishes with locally sourced, seasonal ingredients built through direct relationships with Maui purveyors — a meaningful distinction from restaurants that simply import a mainland concept unchanged.

Open daily from 5 to 11pm for dinner only, the restaurant sits within a full-service luxury resort, with valet parking available and reservations recommended given both the setting and the ongoing demand for a well-known chef's restaurant.

This is squarely a special-occasion or splurge dinner rather than a casual meal, priced and positioned at the top end of Maui's dining scene alongside restaurants like Mama's Fish House and Ko.
