The restaurant at Maui Tropical Plantation has gone through a rebrand worth knowing about before planning a visit: the original "The Mill House" concept, known for farm-to-table dishes drawing on Japanese, Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, and Korean influences, has closed and been replaced by "Cafe O'Lei at The Mill House," operated by the established local Cafe O'Lei restaurant group.

The original concept's standout dishes — roasted beef bone marrow flatbread, Korean kalbi steak with carrot and papaya slaw — reflected a genuinely creative approach to Hawaii's multicultural food heritage, along with a bar program built around tropical, locally grown produce and an on-site coffee roasting operation (Mill House Roasting Co.). Whether the new Cafe O'Lei iteration continues that exact menu and concept is worth confirming directly, since restaurant rebrands often bring meaningful changes.

The setting remains a strong draw regardless of which concept is currently operating: the restaurant sits within Maui Tropical Plantation in Waikapu, with views over the grounds and West Maui Mountains, making it a natural pairing with a plantation tour.
