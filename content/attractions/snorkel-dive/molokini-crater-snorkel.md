Molokini's partially submerged crater creates a natural, current-sheltered snorkeling basin with visibility often past 100 feet. Most boats combine it with a second stop along Maui's south coast.

Because it's a protected Marine Life Conservation District, fish are abundant and used to snorkelers, making it one of the more reliable spots on the island to see larger reef life. Morning departures generally get the calmest water.

Sources: Hawaii DLNR Division of Aquatic Resources
