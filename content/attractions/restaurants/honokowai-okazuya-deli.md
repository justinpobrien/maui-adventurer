Honokowai Okazuya & Deli is a tiny, no-frills carry-out spot tucked into a small strip shopping center near the Times grocery store and Ka'anapali Shores condos, serving Asian-influenced Hawaiian plate lunches at genuinely reasonable prices — generous portions running roughly $10 to $16.

The menu covers meat, fish, or vegetarian entrees, salads, deli sandwiches, and pasta, with spam musubi and fresh poke bowls among the standout local items. It's the kind of unpretentious neighborhood spot that rewards visitors willing to skip the resort dining rooms for something more authentic and considerably cheaper.

Hours split into a lunch window (11am-2:30pm) and a separate dinner window (4:30-8:30pm), Monday through Saturday, with Sundays closed entirely. Cash only is a genuine practical consideration — bring bills rather than assuming card payment will work.
