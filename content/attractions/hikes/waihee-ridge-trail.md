Waihe'e Ridge Trail is widely regarded as one of Maui's best hikes for reward-per-effort — a steady, view-packed climb up a knife-edge ridge in the West Maui Mountains, ending at a grassy overlook with sweeping views back down the valley and out to the coast. Sources vary slightly on exact stats, but expect around 2.2 miles each way with somewhere between 1,200 and 1,450 feet of elevation gain, rated moderately challenging and averaging about three hours round trip.

The trail is free and requires no permit, but access has a practical bottleneck: only about 40 parking stalls exist at the trailhead itself, with a larger dirt overflow lot roughly a mile down the road at the property entrance. The gate opens at 7am and closes at 7pm, and the lot regularly fills before 9am, so an early start isn't optional if driving yourself.

Conditions can shift quickly near the top — clouds frequently roll in over the ridge, bringing mud and reduced visibility even on days that start clear at the trailhead. To reach it, take Kahekili Highway (Highway 340) to Maluhia Road, just past Mendes Ranch.
