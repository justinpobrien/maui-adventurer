Maui Ocean Center in Ma'alaea is the island's premier rainy-day and all-ages attraction — a large aquarium built around Hawaii's own reef ecosystems, featuring a walk-through tunnel tank with sharks and rays, extensive coral reef exhibits, and a dedicated humpback whale exhibit that ties directly into the winter whale-watching season happening just offshore.

Standard admission runs $49.95 for adults (ages 13-64), $39.95 for kids 4-12, and $44.95 for seniors, with children 3 and under free — buying online in advance is meaningfully cheaper than paying at the gate. Admission includes the 3D Sphere Experience, an immersive short film supplementing the live exhibits. Most families should plan around two hours to see everything at a comfortable pace.

Being mostly indoors (with an open-air section as well), it's one of the best options on the island for a rainy day or a midday break from sun exposure, and it pairs naturally with a Behind the Scenes add-on tour for families who want a deeper look at the center's marine rehabilitation work.
