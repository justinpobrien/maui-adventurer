Blue Hawaiian Helicopter Tours gives visitors aerial access to parts of Maui that are otherwise impossible to see up close — remote rainforest valleys, cascading waterfalls, and the sea cliffs of neighboring Molokai — all from a comfortable, air-conditioned helicopter departing Kahului Heliport.

The standard and most popular option is the 50-minute West Maui & Molokai route, priced around $419 per person, covering the West Maui Mountains' interior valleys and Molokai's dramatic north shore cliffs. For travelers wanting a more complete experience, a premium 75-90 minute route adds a landing stop at Ulupalakua Ranch on the slopes of Haleakala, breaking up the flight with time on the ground at a working upcountry ranch.

Given both the popularity of helicopter tours generally and Maui's shifting weather patterns, booking ahead — and ideally choosing an early morning slot for the calmest air and clearest visibility — is worth planning around rather than treating as a same-day activity.
