Ulua Beach is widely considered one of Maui's best all-around snorkeling beaches, thanks to a gently sloped sandy entry that opens into a protected, reef-lined cove. It's an easy beach for less experienced snorkelers to ease into the water, while the rocky area at the north end rewards more confident swimmers with denser reef and better fish variety.

Green sea turtles are a regular sighting, especially around the point at the north end. Facilities are simple but sufficient — free parking, restrooms, and showers — with no lifeguard on duty, so this is a swim-at-your-own-judgment spot despite its generally calm conditions.

Access is via a yellow gate off Hale Ali'i Place, reached from Wailea Alanui Drive past the Andaz Resort. The lot fills early, typically by 9am, but Ulua connects directly to Mokapu and Keawakapu beaches, so a full lot here doesn't mean the day is over — park at Keawakapu's south lot instead and walk over.
