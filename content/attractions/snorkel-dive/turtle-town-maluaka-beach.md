Maluaka Beach, better known by its nickname Turtle Town, sits at the end of Makena Road south of Wailea and earns its name honestly — the fringing reef at the south end of the beach is one of the most reliable spots on Maui for shore-based turtle encounters. A sandy entry makes getting into the water easy, and a short swim out reveals a lively reef packed with tropical fish alongside the resident honu.

The appeal here is genuine accessibility to a good reef without needing a boat, which also means Turtle Town draws a real crowd, both from shore snorkelers and boat tours that stop nearby. The parking lot is small and fills fast, making an early arrival more of a necessity than a suggestion.

Conditions need to be calm for this to be a good outing — there's no lifeguard, and murky or rough water is a sign to skip it rather than push through. As with any turtle encounter, keeping a respectful distance matters both ethically and legally; harassing or touching sea turtles is illegal under state and federal law.
