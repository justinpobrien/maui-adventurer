Aloha Mixed Plate has reopened in its original waterfront location behind Cannery Mall in Lahaina, serving the same straightforward, genuinely local plate lunches that built its reputation before the 2023 wildfire — loco moco, kalua pork, and fresh fish over rice, the kind of food actual Maui residents order rather than a tourist-facing interpretation of it.

Its reopening is part of a broader recovery story along this stretch of oceanfront restaurants, which now includes reopened neighbors Star Noodle, Honu Seafood, Mala Ocean Tavern, and Coco Deck Lahaina — together representing a meaningful return of Lahaina's dining scene following the fire.

A daily happy hour from 2 to 4:30pm offers discounted food and drinks, and the oceanfront setting behind Cannery Mall gives it a genuinely scenic backdrop for what's otherwise an unpretentious, casual meal.
