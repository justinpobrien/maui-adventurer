The Behind the Scenes Tour at Maui Ocean Center in Ma'alaea gives visitors access to areas not visible from the main aquarium galleries — working spaces tied to the center's Marine Institute, where staff explain animal care, rehabilitation work, and the operations that keep the aquarium's exhibits running.

The tour is an add-on to regular admission rather than a separate attraction, priced at $15 per person and running about an hour. It's offered five days a week — Monday, Wednesday, Thursday, Friday, and Saturday — with departures at 11:30am and 1:30pm, and requires a minimum of 5 total participants to operate.

This pairs naturally with a full visit to the main aquarium, giving families or marine-life enthusiasts a deeper, more educational layer beyond the standard self-guided exhibits — worth booking ahead given the participant minimum and limited daily departure windows.
