Ho'okipa Lookout sits just off the Hana Highway about 2 miles east of Paia town, giving an elevated view over Ho'okipa Beach Park — long recognized as one of the premier windsurfing and surfing locations in the world thanks to consistently strong trade winds and powerful wave breaks.

On most days with wind (which is most days), the lookout fills with spectators watching professional-caliber windsurfers, kitesurfers, and surfers execute high-level maneuvers just offshore, and the spot hosts organized windsurfing competitions throughout the year. It's an easy, no-hike stop directly off the highway, making it simple to fit into a Road to Hana itinerary or a standalone Paia-area outing.

Beyond the water sports, Ho'okipa is also a reliable spot to see green sea turtles (honu) resting on the beach below in significant numbers — giving the lookout a dual appeal for both action-sports spectators and wildlife watchers. The park itself has restrooms, showers, picnic tables, and ample parking, unlike many of Maui's more remote viewpoints.
