Maui Ocean Center is a large aquarium in Māʻalaea focused entirely on Hawaiian marine life, from reef fish to sharks and rays, including a walk-through tunnel tank.

It's indoors and fully covered, making it a reliable option on a rainy day or during the hottest part of the afternoon, and the exhibits are paced well for younger kids without feeling rushed.
