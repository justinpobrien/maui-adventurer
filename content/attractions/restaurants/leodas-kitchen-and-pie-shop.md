Leoda's is a small bakery and sandwich counter in Olowalu, roughly midway between Lahaina and Kihei, best known for its hand pies and coconut cream pie.

The menu covers breakfast pastries, sandwiches, and a rotating pie case; seating is limited, so it works well as a to-go stop on a driving day rather than a sit-down meal.
