Eskimo Candy has long operated as a combined seafood market and casual cafe in Kihei, built around fresh Maui fish caught daily by local fishermen. The menu covers a wide range of ahi poke preparations (spicy tuna, shoyu wasabi, and others made fresh throughout the day) alongside fish and chips, fish tacos, coconut shrimp, seafood pasta, poke bowls, and a well-regarded seafood chowder.

Current operating status genuinely needs verification before planning a visit: some recent business listings mark this location as permanently closed, while the restaurant's own website and other directories still describe it as active. This kind of conflicting signal often points to a closure that hasn't fully propagated across review platforms, but it could also reflect an outdated closure notice — either way, confirming directly (a phone call or checking the most recent reviews) is the safer move.

If still operating, reported hours run weekdays only, 11am to 7pm, with weekends closed — notably more limited than most comparable Kihei seafood counters.
