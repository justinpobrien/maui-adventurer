Ali'i Kula Lavender Farm sits at about 4,000 feet elevation on Haleakala's western slope in Kula, offering a cooler-climate alternative to Maui's beach-focused attractions along with sweeping views down toward the coast. The farm grows dozens of lavender varieties adapted to grow at this elevation, alongside protea and other upcountry flowers.

General admission is $5 per person (with discounts to $2 for seniors, kama'aina, and military, and free entry for kids 12 and under), covering self-guided access to the grounds. For a more structured visit, a Guided Walking Tour (30-40 minutes, offered at several set times daily) costs $12, while a Guided Cart Tour covering more ground in about an hour runs $25.

The farm is open Friday through Monday from 10am to 4pm (with some sources noting broader daily hours, so confirming the current schedule before visiting is worthwhile), located at 1100 Waipoli Road. It's a relaxed, photogenic stop that pairs naturally with other upcountry activities like Haleakala sunrise or a Kula-area farm-to-table meal.
