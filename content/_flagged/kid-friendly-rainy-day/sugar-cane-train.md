The Sugar Cane Train, formally the Lahaina, Kaanapali & Pacific Railroad, was once a beloved family attraction offering vintage train rides between Lahaina and Ka'anapali through former sugarcane fields. It should not be treated as an active attraction going forward, and this entry exists mainly to flag its status clearly rather than to recommend a visit.

The railroad had already stopped regular operations back in August 2014, citing financial difficulties, and had scaled back to a limited holiday-season-only schedule (late November through December) in its last years of partial operation. The 2023 Lahaina wildfire then destroyed the Lahaina station, the turntable used to turn locomotives around, and roughly half the track. A second, separate fire in Ka'anapali in August 2025 damaged or destroyed most of the remaining train cars that had survived the first disaster.

As of this writing, the railroad sits abandoned with no announced plans or timeline for restoration. There is no reasonable path to recommending this as a current or near-future family activity.
