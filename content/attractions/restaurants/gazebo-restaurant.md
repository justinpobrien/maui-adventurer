The Gazebo Restaurant at Napili Shores has built a loyal following almost entirely around one dish: generous stacks of macadamia nut pancakes, available in several variations (plain macadamia nut, white chocolate macadamia nut, banana macadamia nut, or pineapple banana macadamia nut), often served with coconut syrup. Loco moco, fried rice, Portuguese sausage, and eggs benedict round out a breakfast menu that runs the full length of the restaurant's hours.

Lunch options — salads, sandwiches, burgers, and local-style plate lunches — become available alongside breakfast items rather than replacing them, since the entire menu is served continuously from open to close. All items fall under $15, keeping this an affordable option despite its prime oceanfront setting right next to Napili Bay.

Hours run 7:30am to 2pm daily, with no dinner service at all. The restaurant's popularity means real waits are common, particularly on weekend mornings — arriving close to opening is the most reliable way to minimize the line.
