The Maui Swap Meet has long been a Saturday-morning institution in Kahului, bringing together local vendors selling fresh produce, flowers, crafts, souvenirs, and a mix of secondhand and new goods in the parking lot of University of Hawaii Maui College. It's a genuinely local shopping experience rather than a tourist-oriented market, though visitors are welcome and it's become a popular stop for those wanting Maui-made goods at reasonable prices.

The market runs Saturdays only, from 7am to 1pm — a narrow window that requires actual planning rather than a spontaneous drop-in on any given day. Office hours for vendor inquiries run Thursday and Friday, 11am to 3pm.

Location is straightforward: the UH Maui College lot just off Kahului Beach Road, directly across Wahinepio Road from the Maui Arts and Cultural Center, making it easy to combine with a visit to that venue if timing allows.
