Ho'okipa Beach Park is Maui's north shore showcase — a world-renowned windsurfing and surfing break that's also, somewhat improbably, the best place on the island to reliably see Hawaiian green sea turtles. Consistent trade winds and powerful, well-shaped waves have made it a bucket-list destination for wind sports athletes for decades, with winter swells occasionally reaching 30 feet and drawing only the most experienced surfers.

The turtle viewing is the beach's other claim to fame. A protected stretch on the eastern end serves as a haul-out spot where honu come ashore to rest, often in view of casual visitors from the bluff-top parking area — no boat or snorkel gear required, just a respectful 10-foot distance.

Facilities are solid: free parking in both an upper lot (with overlook views) and a lower lot (with direct beach access), flush toilets, showers, covered picnic areas, and a lifeguard on duty daily from 8am to 4:30pm. The park closes at 7pm, but that still leaves a good window to catch turtles coming ashore near sunset.
