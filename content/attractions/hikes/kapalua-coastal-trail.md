The Kapalua Coastal Trail links several of West Maui's best beaches along a mostly paved, nearly flat path — running from Kapalua Bay north past Namalu Bay, Oneloa Bay, and Honokahua Bay before ending at D.T. Fleming Beach. At about 1.76 miles one-way (roughly 3.5 miles round trip) with only about 200 feet of elevation gain, it's an easy, family-friendly walk rather than a strenuous hike.

The appeal here is less about the trail itself and more about the string of scenic overlooks and beach access points it connects — a good option for beach-hopping without needing to move a car between each stop. Whales are visible from several vantage points along the bluffs during winter migration season.

Parking is available at either end: the Bay Villas beach access lot near Kapalua Bay, or the D.T. Fleming Beach Park lot to the north. The trail is open from sunrise to sunset with no admission fee, and it's walkable comfortably in either direction depending on where parking is available that day.
