Wailua Iki Falls is easy to confuse with its far more famous namesake, Wailua Falls near mile marker 45, but the two are entirely different waterfalls with entirely different access. Where the main Wailua Falls requires no hiking at all, Wailua Iki sits deeper into a state forest reserve, reached via a wide but rocky hunting road rather than a maintained visitor trail — a roughly 1.5-mile round trip that keeps crowds naturally thin.

The reward for the extra effort is relative solitude in a landscape most Road to Hana visitors never see: a quieter waterfall setting without the tour van traffic that packs the marquee stops along the highway. That said, the informal, unmaintained nature of the access road means uneven footing, occasional overgrowth, and no facilities of any kind.

There's no fee for this hike, but it's best suited to visitors comfortable with rougher, less-defined trails rather than families looking for an easy roadside photo stop — that experience is better found at the main Wailua Falls instead.
