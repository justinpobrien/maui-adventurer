Sandwiched between Kam I and Kam III, Kamaole Beach Park II shares the same golden sand, calm morning water, and lifeguard coverage (roughly 8am-4:30pm) as its neighbors, with one standout feature: ADA-accessible ramps running from the park down to the beach, making it one of the easier sand entries in South Kihei for visitors with mobility needs.

Facilities match the rest of the Kamaole trio — restrooms with changing areas, freshwater showers, volleyball courts, and barbecue stations — but parking works differently here. There's no dedicated lot at Kam II; all parking is street-side on the ocean side of South Kihei Road, which fills up just as fast as the metered lots at Kam I and III.

Like its neighbors, Kam II now falls under the Park Maui metered parking program, so budget for a parking fee unless you're a Hawaii resident with a valid state driver's license.
