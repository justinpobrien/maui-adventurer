The Ohai Trail loops through a stretch of native Hawaiian coastal vegetation near Kapalua, offering an easy, family-friendly walk with reliably good views rather than the strenuous elevation gain of trails like Waihe'e Ridge nearby. At roughly 1.2 to 1.3 miles with only about 183 feet of elevation gain, it's rated easy and typically takes 30 minutes to an hour.

The trail's low-key terrain belies its scenic payoff — rolling hills along Maui's northern shoreline, framed by native plant restoration efforts that give the loop its name (ohai is a native Hawaiian coastal shrub). Winter visitors have a good shot at spotting humpback whales breaching or spouting offshore, adding a bonus to what's already a pleasant walk regardless of season.

No fee or permit is required, and the trail's short length and gentle grade make it accessible to a wide range of fitness levels, including families with kids who might not be up for a longer, steeper hike elsewhere on the island.
