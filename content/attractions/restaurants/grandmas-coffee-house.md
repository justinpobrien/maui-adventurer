Grandma's Coffee House has been a fixture in the small upcountry town of Keokea for more than 30 years, built around organic coffee that's grown and roasted right on-site — a rarity that gives the shop a genuine local identity rather than the generic "coffee shop" label. Homemade baked goods, especially the cinnamon rolls, are consistently the most-praised items on the menu.

Beyond coffee and pastries, the menu covers hot oatmeal, scrambled eggs, and on Sundays a rotating eggs benedict served on a cornmeal waffle, plus rotating lunch specials like spinach lasagna, teriyaki chicken, and beef stew. Sandwiches, soups, and salads round things out, with most items priced under $10.

Open daily from 7am to 2pm, it's a natural stop for anyone driving the upcountry route toward or from Haleakala, offering a genuinely local, unhurried alternative to the more tourist-oriented cafes closer to the coast.
