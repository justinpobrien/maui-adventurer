Two-hour catamaran cruises leave from Kāʻanapali Beach most evenings, sailing parallel to the coast as the sun drops behind the boat. Most include one drink and light pupus.

Boarding is straight off the sand rather than from a harbor, which keeps the whole outing simple. Winter sailings (roughly December through April) have a real chance of humpback whale sightings offshore.

Sources: Maui Sailing operators
