Twin Falls is usually the first waterfall stop on the Road to Hana, and its early position on the drive — plus swimmable pools right off an easy trail — makes it one of the most visited spots on the route. Unlike most of Maui's public trails, Twin Falls sits on privately owned farmland, and the family that owns it manages access, parking, and trail maintenance directly.

Parking costs $10 per car across a lot with about 55 spots, first-come-first-served with no reservation system, so it can fill up by mid-morning during peak season. A network of easy, mostly flat paths leads to a couple of different waterfalls and pools suitable for swimming, making it a good family stop without demanding technical hiking.

Two access quirks are worth planning around: the property closes to non-Hawaii residents on the first Saturday of each month, and it can shut down entirely after heavy rain for safety. Checking the official Twin Falls Maui website before setting out saves a wasted stop.
