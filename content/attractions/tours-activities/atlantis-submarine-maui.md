Atlantis Submarine Maui offers a genuinely different way to see the island's marine life: a real passenger submarine dive to over 100 feet below the surface near Lahaina, passing coral reefs, tropical fish, and the wreck of the Carthaginian, a retired whaling ship replica sunk deliberately as an artificial reef. It's a solid option for travelers who want to see Maui's underwater world without getting in the water themselves.

The full experience runs about 1 hour and 45 minutes door to door, including a shuttle boat ride out to the submarine departure point near Lahaina Harbor, with roughly 45 minutes spent actually submerged. Three daily departures run at 9am, 11am, and 1pm. The submarine is air-conditioned and narrated throughout the dive.

Pricing fluctuates with seasonal promotions — a 10% discount on standard adult and child tickets has been offered for summer 2026 bookings, for example — so checking current rates directly before booking is worthwhile. The one firm requirement is a minimum rider height of 36 inches for children.
