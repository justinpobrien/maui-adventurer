Paia Fish Market is the counter-service answer to Mama's Fish House splurge dining just down the road — fresh, simply prepared fish at prices that make it an easy, repeatable stop rather than an occasion. Order at the counter, grab a spot at one of the shared tables, and expect a casual, communal atmosphere typical of this North Shore town.

The menu keeps things focused: mahi mahi, fish burgers, fish sandwiches, seafood pasta, and blackened preparations, with fish tacos and sandwiches featuring grilled ahi, opah, or opakapaka among the most popular orders. Main courses generally run $12 to $25, making this one of the better value seafood stops in the Paia area.

Open daily from 11am to 9pm, Paia Fish Market works well as a lunch stop between North Shore activities or an easy, unfussy dinner without a reservation requirement.
