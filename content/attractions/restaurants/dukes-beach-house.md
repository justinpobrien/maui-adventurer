Duke's Beach House sits right on the sand at Honua Kai Resort and Spa along Ka'anapali North Beach, offering all-day dining with sweeping Pacific views. The menu leans into Hawaiian-influenced dishes built on fresh, sustainable seafood and premium steaks, with poke rolls, crab and macadamia nut wontons, and banana-leaf steamed fish among the standout orders.

Service runs in clearly defined windows across a long day: breakfast from 7:30 to 11am, lunch from 11am to 4pm, and the Barefoot Bar plus full dinner service from 4 to 9pm. That structure makes it a flexible pick regardless of what time of day a West Maui beach outing wraps up.

As part of the well-known Duke's restaurant group (with sister locations elsewhere in Hawaii), it delivers a reliably solid, resort-casual experience rather than a hidden local gem — a good choice for groups wanting an easy, scenic meal without much research required.
