Cove Park (Cove Beach Park) in Kihei is one of South Maui's most reliable spots for first-time surfers and paddleboarders, thanks to a small protected cove that softens incoming swell into gentle, slow-rolling waves well-suited to learning rather than intimidating beginners.

Surf lessons and board rentals are readily available right across the street, making this an easy pick for families wanting to try surfing without arranging transportation to a separate lesson location. The park area itself includes grassy lawn, paved paths, and shade trees, giving non-surfing family members a comfortable place to relax and watch.

A few practical cautions are worth passing along directly: sharp reef and sea urchins are present in shallow areas, so reef-safe footwear helps, and the lineup gets genuinely crowded given how many surf schools operate here simultaneously. Parking can be tight directly at the break — using the nearby Kalama Park lot and walking about 500 feet over is often the easier option.
