Pua'a Ka'a State Wayside Park, near mile marker 22 on the Hana Highway, is a rare combination on this drive: a genuine swimming waterfall that requires no hiking at all. A short paved path leads directly from the parking lot to a lower pool and falls, with an upper pool — generally the better swimming option — visible just beyond the park's picnic tables.

Facilities here go beyond most Road to Hana stops, including actual restrooms (a genuine rarity along the route) and a shaded picnic area, making this a natural spot to combine a swim with a lunch break. The park itself is free to enter, and parking is free as well, though the lot is compact and fills quickly during the mid-morning to early-afternoon tour van rush.

Timing matters more than almost anything else here: arriving before 9am or after 3pm dramatically improves the odds of having the pools to yourself rather than sharing them with several tour groups at once.
