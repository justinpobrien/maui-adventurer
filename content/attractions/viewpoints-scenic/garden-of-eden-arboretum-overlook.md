Garden of Eden Arboretum, at mile marker 10.5 on the Hana Highway, is a privately maintained 26-acre botanical garden built since 1991 around restoring native and indigenous Hawaiian ecosystems, with over 2 miles of gravel walking trails passing more than 700 botanically labeled plants.

Several overlook points throughout the garden offer views out over the surrounding valley and waterfalls, giving it a different character from a purely educational plant collection — it's as much a scenic stop as a botanical one. The garden has also appeared as a filming location, including scenes from the original Jurassic Park.

Admission runs $20 for visitors 16 and older, $10 for ages 5 to 15, with children under 5 free and discounts available for groups of five or more, military, students, AAA members, and kama'aina. Open daily, generally 8am to 4pm — a worthwhile early stop on the Road to Hana for visitors who enjoy gardens and want a paced, well-maintained walking option rather than a rugged hike.
