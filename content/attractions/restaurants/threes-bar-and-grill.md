Three's Bar & Grill takes a genre-blending approach to its menu, combining Hawaiian, Pacific Rim, and Southwestern influences alongside a full sushi and raw bar — an unusually wide range for a single casual restaurant. Fresh seafood, steaks, salads, and noodles round out a menu built to satisfy varied tastes within one group.

What sets it apart from many Kihei casual spots is the entertainment lineup: live music plays on the patio during breakfast, lunch, and dinner every day, and the calendar includes recurring special events like comedy nights and salsa nights, giving it more of a destination feel than a quick meal stop.

Hours generally run 11am to 9pm (some sources note extended hours to 10pm on certain days), with happy hour from 3 to 6pm offering crafted cocktails and appetizers. This is a good pick for a group wanting both a meal and some evening atmosphere in one stop.
