The path links a string of small, calm beaches past resort grounds and sculpture gardens — an easy way to beach-hop without moving the car.

It runs about 1.5 miles one-way, fully paved and mostly flat, connecting beaches like Mokapu, Ulua, and Polo. Each beach has its own access point and short stretch of sand, so it's easy to pick a favorite and settle in.
