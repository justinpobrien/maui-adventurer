Star Noodle has successfully reopened after the 2023 Lahaina wildfire, now operating from a new Front Street address in the space formerly occupied by Frida's. The restaurant's Asian-inspired share plates — everything from ramen to dim sum — remain the core draw, continuing the innovative, casual-but-refined approach that built its reputation before the fire.

Given the location change, anyone working from older guides or bookmarks should update to the current address (1287 Front St, Lahaina) rather than the pre-fire location. Weekday hours run 4:30 to 9pm, though weekend hours have reportedly shifted and are worth double-checking directly given some inconsistency across current listings.

As one of the earlier Lahaina restaurants to rebuild and reopen, Star Noodle represents part of the broader recovery of Front Street's dining scene, alongside neighbors like Aloha Mixed Plate and Mala Ocean Tavern.
