The Shops at Wailea serves as South Maui's answer to Whalers Village — the retail anchor for the resort corridor along Wailea's coastline, with more than 70 stores spanning global luxury brands, lifestyle retailers, and distinctly Hawaiian shops. Gucci and Bottega Veneta sit alongside more accessible names like Maui Jim, ABC Stores, and Maui Waterwear, giving the center a wider price range than "luxury shopping" alone might suggest.

Beyond retail, the complex includes a solid roster of restaurants, making it a viable dinner destination in its own right rather than purely a shopping stop. Its central location within the Wailea resort district makes it an easy walk or short drive from most of the area's major hotels.

Open daily from 10am to 9pm, the evening hours give it flexibility as an after-dinner stroll option or a rainy-afternoon fallback for anyone staying in South Maui's resort corridor.
