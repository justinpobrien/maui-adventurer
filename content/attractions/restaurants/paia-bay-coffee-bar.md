Paia Bay Coffee & Bar covers a wide range of a day's needs in one spot — good coffee and lattes in the morning, fresh food through the afternoon, and craft cocktails with live entertainment in the evening, all set within a tropical garden that gives the space a more relaxed feel than a typical roadside cafe.

Hours vary slightly by day: Monday runs 7:30am to 9pm, Tuesday through Saturday extends to 10pm, and Sunday wraps up earlier at 6pm. A daily happy hour from 3 to 5pm offers discounted food and drinks, and live music is a regular feature that extends the space's appeal into a proper evening hangout rather than just a coffee stop.

Located right on Paia's main strip along Hana Highway, this works well as a flexible stop — a coffee and pastry before a North Shore beach day, or cocktails and live music to close one out.
