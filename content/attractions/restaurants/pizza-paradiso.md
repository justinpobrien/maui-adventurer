Pizza Paradiso stands out in West Maui for both its genre-blending menu and its unusually late hours. The kitchen covers Italian, Greek, and Middle Eastern favorites side by side — gourmet thin-crust pizzas (including gluten-free options), hearty sandwiches, grilled kabobs, falafel, fresh hummus, and tabouleh — giving groups with varied tastes plenty to choose from without needing separate stops.

Breakfast options, including breakfast burritos and breakfast gyros, extend the menu's range even further, though the standout feature is the restaurant's hours: open daily from 11am until 1:30am, with dedicated late-night service from 9pm on. That makes it one of the few reliable late-night dining options in this part of the island.

A happy hour running noon to 5pm Monday through Friday adds discounted food and drinks, and wine and local craft beer round out the beverage list.
