The Baldwin Home Museum, built in the 1830s and once the oldest surviving home on Maui, is currently closed and not available to visit — a status that has actually worsened recently rather than improved. Heavily damaged in the 2023 Lahaina wildfire, the building underwent stabilization work in March 2026, only for one of its weakened stone walls to collapse during a Kona storm days after that work began.

As a result, the Lahaina Restoration Foundation now plans a full, careful deconstruction and rebuild rather than a simple stabilization and reopening. Workers are removing and cataloguing the original stones for reuse in reconstruction, aiming to preserve as much authentic material as possible. Current projections put completion around 2028.

Given this status, the Baldwin Home should not be recommended as a current visitor attraction. It's included here primarily to document its history and flag its closure clearly, so it isn't mistakenly listed as open in any derived app content. Checking the Lahaina Restoration Foundation's official channels closer to any future visit is the best way to confirm whether reconstruction has progressed.
