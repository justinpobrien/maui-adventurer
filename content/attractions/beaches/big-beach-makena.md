Makena State Park's "Big Beach" runs nearly a mile with no resorts in view — one of the last undeveloped beaches on this coast. Shore break can be strong, especially in summer swell, so it's worth checking conditions before swimming out past the break.

The sand is soft and wide enough that even a crowded weekend rarely feels packed. A short, steep walk over the cinder cone at the north end drops down into Little Beach, a smaller and more secluded cove.
