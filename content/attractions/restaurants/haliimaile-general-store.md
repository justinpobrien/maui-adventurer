Hali'imaile General Store occupies a genuinely historic building — a plantation store that operated from the 1920s through the 1980s before being transformed into a gourmet restaurant in 1988 by Beverly and Joe Gannon. More than 30 years later, it remains one of upcountry Maui's most respected farm-to-table destinations, blending Hawaiian flavors with Asian influences and modern technique.

The lunch menu leans toward approachable but elevated dishes — Wagyu beef burgers with grilled Maui Gold pineapple, fresh ahi, beer-battered fish, mahi mahi preparations, and curry dishes — while dinner steps up further with specialties like Bev's Famous Crab Pizza and crispy roast half-duck.

Located near Makawao on the route up to Haleakala National Park, it works well as a pre- or post-summit dinner stop, open daily for lunch (11am-2:30pm) and dinner (4:30-8pm).
