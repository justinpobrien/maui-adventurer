808 Deli is a two-time local award winner for best sandwiches on Maui, built around a straightforward menu of sandwiches, salads, and daily specials rather than an extensive or gimmicky concept. The Cobb salad with smoky bacon and bleu cheese and the "Belly Dancer" pork belly sandwich are the two most consistently praised items.

One correction worth flagging: research confirms this restaurant's actual address is 2439 South Kihei Road, placing it in Kihei (South Maui) rather than West Maui, where it was originally categorized in this project's checklist. This entry has been moved to reflect the accurate location.

Open daily from 8am to 5pm, it's a solid quick-lunch option — order at the counter, no reservations needed, and reasonably priced for the portion sizes.
