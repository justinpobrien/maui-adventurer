Hasegawa General Store has served the remote town of Hana since 1910, founded by brothers Shoichi and Saburo Hasegawa and remaining a family-run institution ever since. As the primary general store in a town at the very end of the long Road to Hana, it functions as an essential practical stop as much as a cultural landmark — the place to grab gas, groceries, or forgotten supplies before continuing on or heading back.

The store's history includes a genuine setback: a fire destroyed the original building entirely, though the attached gas station suffered only minor damage. Rather than closing permanently, the family renovated the old Hana Theater building and reopened there in August 1991, continuing the business without a long gap. In 2007, the store won the Small Business Association's "Maui Family Business of the Year" award, and it celebrated its 100th anniversary serving the Hana community in 2010.

Open daily from 9:30am to 6pm, it remains both a functional necessity for anyone exploring the Hana area and a small piece of living local history worth a quick stop even for visitors who don't need supplies.
