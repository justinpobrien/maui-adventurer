Hana Ranch Restaurant serves Hana's version of local comfort food — Hawaiian classics, fusion dishes, and what's been called a "world class smash burger" — alongside a full lineup of wine, beer, and specialty cocktails. It's one of the more reliable full-service dining options in a town with genuinely limited restaurant choices given its remote location at the end of the Road to Hana.

The restaurant relocated from its original spot in Hana Town Center into Hana Maui Resort's main dining room, a change dating back to 2021 that's worth knowing given how many older travel guides and directions still reference the previous location.

Open for lunch and dinner daily from 11am to 9pm, it functions as a dependable stop for visitors making the long drive out to Hana who don't want to rely solely on quick roadside food stands for a full sit-down meal at the end of the journey.
