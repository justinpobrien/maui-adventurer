Goofy Foot Surf School has been teaching visitors to surf in Lahaina since 1994, making it the longest-running surf school in town. Founded by Tim Sherer, the school built its reputation on a straightforward promise: a two-hour group lesson that guarantees you'll stand and ride a wave, or the lesson is free.

Lessons run about two hours and are offered in both group and private formats, with group pricing generally starting around $65 to $85 per person depending on current rates and group size. The school is based at 505 Front St, open Monday through Saturday from 7am to 4pm, and closed Sundays.

Given its long track record and central Front Street location, Goofy Foot remains one of the most established options for first-time surfers on Maui's west side, well-suited to complete beginners including kids and less confident swimmers given the gentle beginner breaks typically used for lessons.
