Around mile marker 11 on the Hana Highway, Puohokamoa Falls drops into a swimmable pool just a few minutes from a small paid parking area.

The short, well-graded path makes this an easy add-on rather than a detour, and the on-site picnic tables make it a natural lunch stop partway through the Road to Hana drive.

Sources: Maui County Visitor Information
