Coral Gardens is often cited by boat tour operators as Maui's best snorkeling, and the reasoning holds up: at over 100 acres, it's the island's largest reef system, tucked along the southern coast of West Maui between Olowalu and Ma'alaea and protected by surrounding cliff lines that shield it from wind and rough seas.

Because it's accessible only by boat, Coral Gardens has avoided the wear that heavier foot traffic brings to shore-access reefs, and the payoff is a genuinely pristine underwater landscape. Expect triggerfish, angelfish, parrotfish, and butterflyfish in large numbers, along with occasional sightings of Hawaiian monk seals, sea turtles, octopus, and eels — a wider mix of marine life than many single shore spots offer.

There's no way to reach this site independently; it's built into snorkel tour itineraries run by operators like Four Winds Maui and similar companies, often as part of a half-day trip that may also include other stops. Pricing and duration vary by operator, making this one to book in advance rather than plan around on the fly.
