Ko Restaurant, inside the Fairmont Kea Lani in Wailea, builds its entire concept around Maui's multicultural plantation history — specifically "kaukau time," the shared midday meals that brought together Hawaiian, Filipino, Portuguese, Korean, Japanese, and Chinese sugarcane workers generations ago. Chef Matt Dela Cruz's menu translates that heritage into a genuinely distinctive dining experience rather than a generic "Hawaii Regional Cuisine" label.

Dishes range roughly $19 to $33, putting it solidly in upscale-casual territory rather than the top tier of Wailea resort pricing. Dinner is served daily from 5 to 9pm, with a Sunday brunch option from 10am to 12:30pm offering a different, lighter way to sample the kitchen's approach.

The resort setting means reservations are worth making in advance, particularly for dinner during peak travel periods, though the restaurant's cultural-history angle sets it apart from the more generic fine-dining options elsewhere in Wailea.
