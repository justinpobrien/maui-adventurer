Bailey House Museum, formally known as Hale Ho'ike'ike, occupies a former missionary residence in Wailuku built in the 1830s, and today houses the Maui Historical Society's collection covering pre-contact Hawaiian culture, missionary-era history, and the island's plantation era — a compact but substantive stop for history-minded families.

The museum keeps limited hours, open Tuesday through Friday from 10am to 2pm only, closed Saturday through Monday — a detail worth building into trip planning rather than discovering at the door. Admission is modest at $5 for adults, $1 for children ages 7-12, and $4 for seniors, located at 2375 Main Street in Wailuku.

Given the narrow visiting window, this works best paired with other central Maui stops on the same weekday, such as Iao Valley or Kepaniwai Heritage Gardens nearby, rather than as a standalone destination.
