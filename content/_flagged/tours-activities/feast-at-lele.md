Feast at Lele, once a well-regarded oceanfront luau on Lahaina's Front Street known for pairing a multi-course dinner with dance performances representing several Polynesian cultures, was destroyed in the August 8, 2023 Lahaina wildfire. The venue at 505 Front Street was lost entirely.

The business's own official statement confirms it is "no longer accepting reservations," acknowledging the loss directly: "The Feast at Lele was among the many homes and businesses destroyed in the Lahaina wildfires... we thank our valued guests and supporters, and look forward to the possibility of welcoming you in the future." No reopening timeline has been announced as of this writing.

This entry should not be treated as a bookable attraction going forward. Visitors interested in a comparable Lahaina-area luau experience should consider Old Lahaina Luau (which survived the fire and continues operating) or Drums of the Pacific Luau at the Hyatt Regency in Ka'anapali, both confirmed active as of 2026.
