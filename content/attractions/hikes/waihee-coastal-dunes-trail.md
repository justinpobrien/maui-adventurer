The Waihe'e Coastal Dunes and Wetlands Refuge offers a flat, easy alternative to the strenuous ridge hike sharing its name nearby — a roughly 2-mile round trip trail across coastal dunes and wetlands managed by the Hawaiian Land Trust. Unlike Waihe'e Ridge, this is a gentle walk suitable for families and casual hikers, ending at Waihe'e Stream with ocean views along the way.

The land carries real ecological and cultural weight: it's home to endangered native Hawaiian birds and plants, and the refuge protects one of the few remaining coastal dune ecosystems on Maui in something close to its natural state. Views along the route take in the West Maui Mountains inland and powerful open-ocean waves crashing against the rocky shoreline.

Access is informal — park at the public Waihe'e Municipal Golf Course and follow the dirt road in, watching for a trail sign near a dark green house that marks the actual start. The lower portion of the loop is fully exposed with little shade, so sun protection and water matter even on a short, flat walk like this one.
