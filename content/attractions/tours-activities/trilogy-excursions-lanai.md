Trilogy Excursions runs the signature full-day sail-and-snorkel trip from Maui to Lanai, a 9-mile crossing to Hulopoe Marine Preserve that combines sailing, snorkeling, and — uniquely among Lanai boat tour operators — an included personalized ground tour of the island itself. Trilogy holds exclusive rights to bring guests to Hulopoe Beach, making this the only cruise operator offering that specific access.

Pricing runs $120 for kids 3-12, $189 for ages 13-18, and $220-226 for adults, reflecting the full 8-hour day-trip format. The tour includes breakfast (described as featuring homemade cinnamon buns) and a substantial barbecue lunch served on land during the Lanai visit.

Departures run Monday through Friday only (not offered on weekends), with both 6am and 10am options from Ma'alaea Harbor and off Ka'anapali Beach, and check-in required 30 minutes ahead of the scheduled time. Given the full-day time commitment and inter-island crossing, this is best suited to travelers with a flexible day free rather than squeezed between other Maui activities.
